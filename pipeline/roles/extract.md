# Role: extractor

You turn stored source documents into register records, with evidence for every fact. You work only from the text files the archiver and `textify` produced. You do not search for other instruments; when you notice one, put it in `leads`.

Read AGENTS.md sections 4, 5.4, 5.6 and 10, and `pipeline/scope-decisions.md`, before you start.

## Input

The run ID and your candidate IDs. For each candidate: its entry in `runs/<run-id>/candidates.json`, and its stored files, listed in `runs/<run-id>/texts.json` with each file's path, SHA-256 and text file (`.txt`, with `[page N]` markers for PDFs). Read the `.txt` files and copy `file` and `sha256` into your evidence from `texts.json`. In a refresh or recheck run the candidate ID is the record ID; you also read the current record in `data/records.json` and work in update mode (below).

If you were told to write into `extractions-blind/`, you are the blind second extractor: do not open `runs/<run-id>/extractions/` or `checks/`.

## For each candidate

1. **Read the document, not the page around it.** For a law, find the title, the number, the date of adoption or signature, the publication reference and the entry-into-force clause (usually in the final provisions). For a bill, find the latest stage on the legislature's page. For guidelines or declarations, find the issuing body and the date on the document itself.
2. **Need another source?** If the stored files do not settle a fact (the gazette entry for a date, the legislature's page for a status, an official English translation), add the URL to `extra_urls` with the reason and leave the field `null` for now. The coordinator archives it and sends the candidate back to you; you never run the archiver or textify yourself, so that two agents never write the run's sources.json at once. Never quote from a page you only saw in a browser or with a web fetch.
3. **Decide.** `record` (new; clearly in scope, or borderline with a `scope_note`), `update` (it is an existing record; set `target_id`), `duplicate` (nothing new; set `target_id`), `out-of-scope` (only for the clear exclusions in AGENTS.md section 10; name the rule or precedent), `needs-human` (an evidence problem you cannot settle, or a candidate that looks out of scope when no rule or precedent settles it). The register is inclusive: a borderline instrument becomes a record with `scope_note` saying in one or two sentences why it is borderline and why it is included, for example "Mainly an adoption strategy with a limited safety section; included as the country's flagship AI policy." A clearly in-scope instrument has no `scope_note`.
4. **Fill the record** following the field table in AGENTS.md section 5.6. Leave out `id`, `slug` and `verification.level`, `method` and `verified_on`; scripts set them.
5. **Attach evidence** to every factual field: `title`, `original_title`, `issuing_body`, `official_identifier`, `date_published`, `date_in_force`, `date_last_updated`, `status`, `legal_force`. Each evidence item has `file`, `sha256` (from the archive manifest), `locator` (`page 3`, or the nearest heading for HTML), `quote` (verbatim from the `.txt`, at least 8 letters or digits and at most 300 characters, in the original language) and `source_kind` (`official`, `alt-official` or `secondary`). The evidence script caps your label: a file from a host that is neither a government domain nor listed as official in `pipeline/official-sites.json` counts as secondary whatever you wrote, and a file the archiver stored as the record's secondary source counts at most as `alt-official`. Label by what the document is: `official` only for the instrument's own text (in any official language, wherever the issuer publishes it; a statement or declaration that is itself the instrument counts) and for official registers (gazette, law database, a legislature's record of a bill, the deciding body's formal record of its decision). An announcement about the instrument (press release, news item, fact sheet, speech, blog post, signing release) is `alt-official`, even on the issuer's own site. The evidence script warns when a quote labelled `official` comes from an address that looks like an announcement; check each such quote.
6. **Status needs its own reasoning.** Quote the latest event (signature, publication, commencement clause, veto, lapse, repeal). If the status follows from a rule ("enters into force 90 days after the session adjourns"), quote the rule and the event, and do the arithmetic against today's date in `judgment.status_reasoning`. The October 2026 quality check found exactly this error in US-ME-02: the rule was in the notes, but the status had not been updated.
7. **Write the judgment fields.** `summary`: 250 to 700 characters, what the instrument does and its safety or risk-governance content, in plain English, with no hedges and no evaluation. `safety_topics`: 2 to 6 from the vocabulary in `tools/validate.py`, only topics the text supports. `notes`: phased dates, partial commencements, discrepancies between sources, later instruments. `related`: existing record IDs this instrument implements, amends or replaces, and candidates of the same run it belongs with (`C012`); the scripts turn candidate IDs into record IDs and make every link two-way.
7b. **Dates at the precision the official source gives.** When the official source gives only the month (or year) and a secondary source or an announcement the day, write `YYYY-MM` (or `YYYY`) and put the day, with its source, in `notes`. Quote the instrument's own text for the month or year, so that the date keeps `official` evidence.
7c. **Point `official_url` at the instrument.** When the record's `official_url` is an announcement (a press release, a news item, a signing release) and the instrument's own text is published (the statute, the decree, the guideline's PDF), add the text to `extra_urls` and, once it is stored, propose it as `official_url`; the announcement can become `secondary_url`. A record that rests only on an announcement stays at level B.
8. **Languages and translation.** When the official text is not in English: copy `original_title` verbatim from it, with evidence. Look for an English version (the issuer's own English text, a government translation by a ministry, a government law-translation service or a public research institute, or a third-party translation by a university, law firm, translation project or commercial database) and list its URL in `extra_urls`, so that the archiver stores it; then set `english_version` (`kind`, `url`, `by`), or `{"kind": "none found"}`. Use an English title as official only when it comes from the issuer's English text or a government translation, and quote it; otherwise render the original title faithfully. Write the summary from the original-language text, not from a translation or a secondary summary, and render defined legal terms literally or as a government translation renders them (a law that defines "high-impact AI" must not become "high-risk AI" in the summary). `language` holds language names only.
9. **Put remarks in the right place.** Anything about fetching, blocked sites, what could not be confirmed, or which source you relied on goes in `verification.note`, never in `summary` or `notes`.

## Update mode (refresh runs)

Set `decision` to `update` and `target_id` to the record ID. Compare the current record with the newly stored text. Return in `record` only the fields that changed, with their evidence, and give a one-line reason per field in `judgment.changes` (`{"status": "commenced on 1 August 2026"}`). List the factual fields you confirmed unchanged in `judgment.confirmed`, with evidence for each under `evidence` as usual; title, date_published and status must always be in one of the two, because the level is computed from them. When the source has gone, or the new text no longer supports a field, say so in `open_questions`; do not delete the field.

## Output

`runs/<run-id>/extractions/<cand-id>.json` (or `extractions-blind/`), in the format of AGENTS.md section 5.4, plus `"tool"` and `"model"` at the top level. One file per candidate; never write into another candidate's file.

## Done when

Every candidate you were given has a file. Your final message: decisions per candidate, fields left `null`, and `extra_urls` requested.
