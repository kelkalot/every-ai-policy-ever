Summary

Creates, within the national transparency and data-protection authority, a programme to promote analysis, regulation and state capacity-building for the development and use of AI in the public and private sectors while guaranteeing rights to transparency and personal-data protection. It has three components: an AI observatory (mapping actors, tracking regional and global regulation, periodic reports); governance and participation (a multidisciplinary advisory council and participatory drafting of regulatory strategies against rights violations and harmful AI impacts); and capacity-building (good-practice guides, training, awareness campaigns, and documentation of automated decision systems).

Status notes

Signed on 30 Aug 2023 and published in the Boletín Oficial on 4 Sep 2023 (no. 35247, p. 17). The AAIP's national directorates for transparency policy evaluation and for personal data protection carry out the programme jointly and set up mechanisms to monitor and evaluate it (Art. 2). The annex also provides for publishing the transparency criteria of automated decision systems on the National Transparency Portal. The recitals cite the OECD AI Principles, the UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI and the Recommendations for a Trustworthy AI approved by Disposición 2/2023 (AR-01). The resolution sets no date of entry into force.
