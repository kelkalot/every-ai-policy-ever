Summary

Amends Arkansas Code § 25-1-128 to require every taxpayer-funded public entity to create an artificial intelligence and automated decision tool policy. The policy must define the authorized use of AI and of automated decision tools (systems using AI developed or modified to make, or be a controlling factor in, consequential decisions) and require an authorized human employee or designee to make any final decision, whatever the tool recommends. Employees must be trained on the appropriate use of such tools in deciding outcomes, state entities' policies must be available to the public on request, and public entities must set a disciplinary procedure for violations.

Status notes

Filed on 1 Apr 2025; passed the House on 7 Apr 2025 and the Senate on 10 Apr 2025 with Senate Amendment No. 1, which only added Sen. J. English as a cosponsor; the House concurred on 14 Apr 2025 and the bill became Act 848, approved on 17 Apr 2025. The Act has no emergency clause or specified effective date, so it took effect on 5 Aug 2025, the effective date of such acts of the 2025 regular session, which adjourned sine die on 5 May 2025. It amends § 25-1-128 as already amended by Act 205 of 2025. Each policy must also prohibit using a public entity's technology resources to express personal political opinions to elected officials or lobby them (with exceptions), to engage in illegal activities or to override security procedures. The Department of Education, with the State Cybersecurity Office, must develop the technology resources and AI and automated decision tool policies used by each type of state educational institution.
