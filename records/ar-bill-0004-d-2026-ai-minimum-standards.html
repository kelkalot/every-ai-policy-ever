Presupuestos Mínimos para la Promoción del desarrollo de la Inteligencia Artificial (IA) en la República Argentina

Summary

Framework bill declaring AI development of national strategic interest and setting minimum standards for AI providers, developers and operators in Argentina. Risk is graded high, moderate or minimal by use, transparency to users, personal-data handling and human-rights impact. Principles include robustness and safety with continuous evaluation, transparency and accountability. Those covered must avoid biometric categorisation inferring sensitive traits and manipulative systems, answer complaints, report data breaches and obtain informed consent; generative-AI operators must work towards watermarks, labels or metadata for realistic synthetic content. It creates an AI promotion council.

Status notes

Entered in Trámite Parlamentario No. 1 of 2 Mar 2026 and referred to the committees on General Legislation, on science (the Chamber's list abbreviates the name) and on Human Rights and Guarantees. The bill's explanatory statement says it re-presents bill 4079-D-2024, which had lapsed, and frames it in the OECD AI Principles. The Council for the Promotion and Development of AI would sit within the Cabinet Office, with one ad honorem representative of each ministry, meet at least quarterly and report each year to Congress on how the law is applied (Arts. 10-11).
