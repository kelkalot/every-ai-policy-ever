Summary

Australia's core guidance for the responsible adoption of AI across the economy, for both developers and deployers of AI systems. It sets out 6 practices aligned with Australia's AI Ethics Principles and international standards: decide who is accountable, understand impacts and plan accordingly, measure and manage risks, share essential information, test and monitor, and maintain human control. It comes as Foundations, for organisations such as small and medium-sized enterprises and not-for-profits, and as Implementation practices for governance professionals and technical experts.

Status notes

Published in October 2025 as the first update of the Voluntary AI Safety Standard of September 2024. The update condensed the standard's 10 guardrails into 6 essential practices, removed redundant language and extended the guidance to AI developers as well as deployers; an appendix maps each practice of the standard to the new implementation practices. Supporting tools referred to in the guidance include AI screening questions, a triage template and an AI register template.
