Summary

Whole-of-government policy that sets mandatory requirements for the use of AI by all non-corporate Commonwealth entities, with some exceptions. Version 2.0, effective 15 December 2025, requires departments and agencies to have accountable officials, publish AI transparency statements, develop a strategic approach to AI adoption, operationalise the responsible use of AI, assign accountability for each AI use case, keep internal use case registers, train staff on AI and carry out AI use case impact assessments. It aims to keep government an exemplar in responsible AI use.

Status notes

Version 2.0, the December 2025 update, took effect on 15 December 2025 and replaced version 1.1, which took effect on 1 September 2024. The update added requirements for a strategic approach to AI adoption, for operationalising the responsible use of AI, for designated accountability for AI use cases and for risk-based actions at the level of each use case. The policy is organised in three sections: strategy and oversight, preparedness and operations, and AI use case impact assessment. The DTA publishes with it a technical standard for government's use of AI, an agentic AI addendum, an AI impact assessment tool and the APS AI Plan 2025.
