Summary

Set 10 voluntary guardrails for organisations across the AI supply chain: an accountability process, risk management, data governance and protection of AI systems, testing and monitoring, meaningful human oversight, informing end-users about AI use and AI-generated content, processes to challenge AI outcomes, transparency across the supply chain, record-keeping, and stakeholder engagement focused on safety and fairness. It described what developers and deployers should do to use AI safely and used language aligned with international standards.

Status notes

Published by the National Artificial Intelligence Centre on 5 September 2024 as the first version of Australia's AI safety standard. On 21 October 2025 the Centre published the Guidance for AI Adoption, which the DISR page describes as updated and simplified guidance for industry that evolves the standard. The Guidance calls itself the first update of the standard: it condensed the 10 guardrails into 6 essential practices, extended the guidance to AI developers as well as deployers, and integrated all of the standard's practices, with a crosswalk from each guardrail to the new practices.
