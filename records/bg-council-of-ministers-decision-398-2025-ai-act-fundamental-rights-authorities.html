Решение на Министерския съвет № 398 от 18.06.2025 г.

Summary

Decision of the Bulgarian Council of Ministers under Article 77(2) of the AI Act designating seven bodies as national authorities that supervise or ensure compliance with EU-law obligations protecting fundamental rights, including non-discrimination, in the use of the high-risk AI systems listed in Annex III: the Ombudsman, the Central Election Commission, the Commission for Protection against Discrimination, the Commission for Personal Data Protection, the Consumer Protection Commission, the State Agency for Child Protection and the General Labour Inspectorate Executive Agency. The Minister of e-Government is to notify the Commission and the other Member States of the list and publish it.

Status notes

Adopted at the Council of Ministers' sitting of 18 June 2025 and entered in the Council of Ministers' register of acts on 19 June 2025. It designates only fundamental-rights authorities. In answers to BTA published on 9 January 2026, the Ministry of e-Government, the lead ministry for the AI Act, said that a draft decision naming the Bulgarian Accreditation Service as notifying authority had not yet been submitted to the Council of Ministers, that a draft decision on market surveillance had yet to go through interministerial coordination, that no decisions had been taken on the coordination model or the sanctions regime, and that the national legal framework was due by March 2026. A private members' bill with a fuller framework was filed in the National Assembly on 24 September 2026 (BG-02).
