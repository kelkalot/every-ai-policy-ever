Summary

Declaration by 28 countries and the EU on the risks of 'frontier' AI: highly capable general-purpose models, and narrow AI with harmful capabilities, that match or exceed today's most advanced models. It warns of substantial risks from intentional misuse or unintended issues of control, names cybersecurity, biotechnology and disinformation, and sees potential for serious, even catastrophic, harm. Its agenda is a shared scientific understanding of these risks and risk-based national policies, with safety testing, evaluations and transparency from developers of frontier AI, and an international network of scientific research on frontier AI safety.

Status notes

Agreed at the AI Safety Summit at Bletchley Park, 1-2 November 2023; the countries represented include the US, China and the EU. New Zealand joined the commitment on 23 October 2024, and GOV.UK updated the publication on 13 February 2025 to include it as a signatory. The participants said they would meet again in 2024. The International AI Safety Report (interim report INT-08, full report INT-09) was launched at this summit. GOV.UK also offers a Latin American Spanish version of the publication.
