Summary

Thirty-one-article bill to guarantee and promote the responsible implementation, use and supervision of AI systems affecting persons in Bolivia. It sets principles of transparency, safety and non-discrimination; grants rights not to be subject to solely automated decisions and to human oversight; requires providers to inform users of AI use and run post-market monitoring plans; prohibits subliminal manipulation, exploitation of vulnerabilities and social scoring; defines general-purpose models with systemic risk; makes developers and users liable for negligent harm; and makes AGETIC the regulator with graduated sanctions.

Status notes

The Chamber of Senators approved the text on 22 Oct 2025, and its President transmitted it the same day to the Chamber of Deputies under Article 163(5) of the Constitution; the Chamber received it on 29 Oct 2025. By note of 27 Apr 2026 a deputy asked the Chamber's President, under Article 117 of its General Rules, to reinstate ('reposición') the bill so that its treatment continues in the current legislative year; the Chamber lists it among bills under review. The bill also provides fiscal, financial and administrative incentives for AI research and innovation (Article 15), requires the Executive to regulate it within 90 days of publication and to amend Supreme Decree No. 2514 of 9 Sep 2015, and gives AGETIC 180 days to issue technical guidelines and the national AI strategy.
