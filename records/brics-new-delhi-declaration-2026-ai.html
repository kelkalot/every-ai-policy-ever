Summary

Declaration of the 18th BRICS summit (New Delhi, 12-13 September 2026), in 140 paragraphs. Paragraph 81 recognises AI's potential for growth and sustainable development and says that international cooperation on access to AI resources 'while ensuring its safety, security, inclusiveness and reliability', on energy-efficient and trustworthy AI and on mitigating potential risks is essential, especially for the Global South; it commits members to implement the 2025 BRICS Leaders' Statement on the Global Governance of AI. Paragraph 48 calls for efforts against malicious software, cybercrime, disinformation and deepfakes.

Scope

Borderline scope A general BRICS summit declaration whose AI content is limited to a few paragraphs; included because paragraph 81 commits members to implement the 2025 BRICS statement on AI governance (BRICS-01).

Status notes

The text is dated New Delhi, 12 September 2026; South Africa's government news service reported on 13 September 2026 that the summit had concluded with the leaders adopting the declaration. Paragraph 81 also acknowledges India's AI Impact Summit (February 2026) and the World AI Conference and High-Level Meeting on Global AI Governance in Shanghai. Paragraph 95 welcomes work on AI and other new technologies in the financial sector, including the BRICS Fintech Working Group's assessment of their opportunities and risks. It should not be confused with the New Delhi Declaration of the February 2026 AI Impact Summit (INT-02). Paragraph 140 supports China's BRICS Chairship in 2027.
