Summary

Principles-based guide by Brunei's Authority for Info-communications Technology Industry (AITI), developed with an AI Governance and Ethics Working Group, for organisations that design, develop, deploy or use AI. It sets seven principles: transparency and explainability; data protection and data governance; security and safety; robustness and reliability; fairness and equity; human centricity; and accountability and integrity. Its governance framework covers people, process and technology: internal governance roles, risk impact assessments that set the level of human involvement, lifecycle risk management, testing, security safeguards and incident response plans.

Status notes

AITI set up the AI Governance and Ethics Working Group in May 2024 with 25 members from government, industry and academia (the guide lists 24 member organisations) and released a public consultation paper on the draft guide on 9 July 2024, which drew feedback from 17 respondents. The guide describes itself as a baseline of considerations and safeguards that organisations in any sector may extend with sector-specific measures, and says it may be amended periodically with prior notice. Its principles align with the ASEAN Guide on AI Governance and Ethics, and it names ISO/IEC 42001:2023 and Singapore's AI Verify as examples of a management-system standard and a testing framework. The guide carries an AITI 2024 copyright line. AITI's AI governance page also lists a 'Second Edition' of the guide, without a date.
