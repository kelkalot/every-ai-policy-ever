Summary

Requires developers of generative AI systems or services made available to Californians to post on their websites documentation of the data used to train them, including a high-level summary of the datasets used: for example, whether they include data protected by copyright, trademark or patent, or are entirely in the public domain. Developers had to post the documentation on or before 1 Jan 2026 for systems released on or after 1 Jan 2022.

Status notes

Approved by the Governor and chaptered on 28 Sep 2024 as Chapter 817, Statutes of 2024. It adds Title 15.2 (commencing with Section 3110) to Part 4 of Division 3 of the Civil Code; the posting duty is in Civil Code section 3111. The Governor's release of 29 Sep 2024 lists it among the 17 bills on generative AI that he had signed over the previous 30 days. Compliance required from 1 Jan 2026.
