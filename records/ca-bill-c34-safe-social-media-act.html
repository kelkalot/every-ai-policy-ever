Summary

Online-harms bill creating a Digital Safety Act and Digital Safety Commission. AI chatbot services are expressly regulated, with a duty to protect children and a chatbot-specific duty to act responsibly (reduce risks of harmful content and behaviour, emergency measures in crises); social media services must label synthetically generated content and make non-consensual intimate content, including sexual deepfakes, inaccessible.

Status notes

First reading in the House of Commons on 10 June 2026 (sitting 133). LEGISinfo lists the bill as at second reading in the House of Commons, with no second-reading activity, no recorded votes and no major speeches. The Digital Safety Commission would set details of the obligations by regulation, including age estimation and verification and design features such as labelling tools; for social media services the government intends a minimum age of 16 for accounts. The bill was informed by the Expert Advisory Group on Online Safety, reconvened from March to May 2026.
