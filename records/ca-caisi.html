Summary

Launches CAISI within ISED with an initial CAD 50 million over five years to study the risks of advanced AI systems, develop measures to address them, and collaborate with AI safety institutes in other countries; research runs through a CIFAR-administered stream and NRC-led government-directed projects.

Status notes

One component of the CAD 2.4 billion AI investment announced in Budget 2024. The institute is led by ISED and draws on the National Research Council, CIFAR and the three national AI institutes (Amii, Mila and the Vector Institute). Its page names as its focus the risks of advanced AI systems, including synthetic content used for impersonation and fraud and systems that may be dangerous or hinder human oversight; its government-directed projects include joint testing with international counterparts and guidance to AI developers, users and the public. Canada is a founding member of the International Network of AI Safety Institutes. Under its international role, the institute's page lists the Network's mission statement, a Research Agenda on Risks from Synthetic Content and a memorandum of understanding with Australia on AI safety collaboration; its first blog post, on reporting AI evaluations, appeared on 8 July 2026. Canada's National Artificial Intelligence Strategy: AI for All, launched on 4 June 2026, commits a further CAD 50 million to expand its capabilities to track emerging AI risks, advance technical research and conduct transparent evaluations of AI models.
