Summary

Mandatory Treasury Board directive for federal institutions that use automated decision systems, including machine learning, to make or assist administrative decisions about clients. Before production, institutions must complete and publish an Algorithmic Impact Assessment that sets one of four impact levels and apply that level's requirements: notice and explanation, peer review, Gender-based Analysis Plus, staff training, testing and monitoring for bias and unfair outcomes, human involvement (a human makes the final decision at levels III and IV), approval up to Treasury Board, recourse and public reporting.

Status notes

Binding on the institutions subject to the Policy on Service and Digital, under whose authority it is issued; other departments and agencies are encouraged to meet it as good practice. Launched on 4 March 2019, it took effect on 1 April 2019, with compliance required by 1 April 2020, and applies to systems developed or procured after that date. Amended on 1 April 2021, on 25 April 2023 (third review: expanded scope and new measures on explanation, bias testing, data governance, GBA Plus and peer review) and on 24 June 2025 (fourth review: transparency of systems and their impacts, senior-level accountability, testing and monitoring; the Algorithmic Impact Assessment tool was updated with it). Systems developed or procured before 24 June 2025, and agents of Parliament, had until 24 June 2026 to comply with the new or updated requirements. The directive is reviewed every two years.
