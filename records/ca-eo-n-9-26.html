Executive Order N-9-26

Summary

Speeds up the independent-verification framework under SB 813 and AB 1405. It orders the Government Operations Agency, with Cal OES and national experts, to recommend by 16 Nov 2026 whether to amend state AI safety laws to require: onsite independent verification organisations at large frontier labs; independent verification of safety frameworks, transparency reports and risk assessments; a verified 'kill switch' for frontier models; and loss-of-control incidents within reportable critical safety incidents.

Status notes

Signed 18 Sep 2026. The press release cites recent AI incidents, including a 'Hugging Face attack', described as a loss-of-control incident, and notes the absence of federal incident-reporting law. Deadlines: recommendations by 16 Nov 2026; IVO application criteria by 1 May 2027; Gov. Code sec. 11549.82 actions by 1 Dec 2027. On 23 Sep 2026 the Governor named the experts who will advise on the recommendations: Jason Goldman, Gillian Hadfield, Alondra Nelson and Rob Reich. Binding on state agencies only; it does not itself amend SB 53.
