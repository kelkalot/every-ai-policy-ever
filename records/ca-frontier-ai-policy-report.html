Summary

Report of an expert working group that the Governor asked in September 2024 to help California develop guardrails for frontier AI. Without endorsing any bill, it sets out evidence-based policy principles in a 'trust but verify' approach: transparency on data acquisition, safety and security practices, pre-deployment testing and downstream impacts; independent verification of safety claims and risk assessments; whistleblower protections and safe harbours for third-party evaluators; adverse-event reporting to monitor post-deployment harms; and thresholds for intervention, based for example on compute or users, that can be revised over time.

Status notes

Draft released on 18 Mar 2025 for public feedback until 8 Apr 2025; final report 17 Jun 2025. Co-led by Jennifer Tour Chayes, Mariano-Florentino Cuéllar and Fei-Fei Li, with Rishi Bommasani and Scott R. Singer as lead writers; the co-leads write in their own capacity, not for their institutions. Requested in September 2024, the month of the Governor's veto of SB 1047 (US-CA-03), it focuses on frontier models and leaves out AI's effects on work, the environmental impact of data centres and the full range of misuse. SB 53 (2025) built on its recommendations.
