Chapter 677, Statutes of 2025 (Senate Bill 243)

Summary

Requires operators of companion chatbot platforms to disclose that interactions are artificially generated and to keep protocols for detecting and responding to suicidal ideation and self-harm, including crisis referrals. For minors it requires break reminders and blocks sexually explicit images. Operators must report self-harm protocols and crisis-referral statistics to public health authorities, and families have a private right of action against noncompliant and negligent developers.

Status notes

Passed the Assembly 59-1 on 10 Sep 2025 and the Senate 33-3 on 11 Sep 2025; signed on 13 Oct 2025 as Chapter 677, Statutes of 2025, in a package on children's online safety that also included AB 1043 (age verification signals), AB 316 (artificial intelligence: defenses), AB 621 (deepfake pornography) and AB 853 (California AI Transparency Act amendments). Effective 1 Jan 2026. On 10 Sep 2026 the Governor signed SB 1119 ('Companion chatbots: children's safety') and SB 867 ('Toys: companion chatbots'); his release says the 2025 protections for AI companion chatbots were strengthened again that day.
