Summary

SB 813 (McNerney) creates a state framework for certifying 'independent verification organizations' that can assess AI models and systems for compliance with state law, codifying a central recommendation of the frontier AI working group. AB 1405 (Bauer-Kahan) creates a state registry of AI auditors with standards for independence, transparency and integrity.

Status notes

Both approved by the Governor on 9 Sep 2026: SB 813 as Chapter 179 and AB 1405 as Chapter 178, Statutes of 2026. SB 813 adds Chapter 14 (commencing with Section 8898) to Division 1 of Title 2 of the Government Code, and AB 1405 adds Chapter 5.9.5 (commencing with Section 11549.80) to Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2. EO N-9-26 of 18 Sep 2026 (US-CA-09) orders the Government Operations Agency to complete the requirements of Gov. Code sec. 8898.1 and post application requirements, procedures and criteria for independent verification organizations by 1 May 2027, and to complete the requirements of Gov. Code sec. 11549.82(a) by 1 Dec 2027. Combined into one record because they were enacted together as a single verification and audit framework.
