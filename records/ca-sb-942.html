Summary

Requires providers of generative AI systems with over 1,000,000 monthly users that are publicly accessible in California to offer a free AI detection tool, to give users the option of a visible (manifest) disclosure, and to embed a latent disclosure in AI-generated image, video and audio content that conveys the provider, the system and version, the time of creation and a unique identifier. Violations carry a civil penalty of $5,000 each. As amended by AB 853 (2025), large online platforms must let users inspect provenance data from 2027, and capture-device makers must offer and by default embed latent disclosures from 2028.

Status notes

Signed on 19 Sep 2024 as Chapter 291, Statutes of 2024, and originally to become operative on 1 Jan 2026. AB 853 (Wicks), Chapter 674, Statutes of 2025, signed on 13 Oct 2025, strengthened the Act; as amended, the covered-provider duties apply from 2 Aug 2026, the duties of large online platforms from 1 Jan 2027 and those of capture-device makers, for devices first produced for sale from that date, from 1 Jan 2028. In his AB 853 signing message the Governor asked the Legislature for follow-up legislation in 2026, before the law took effect, on technical feasibility and user privacy. AB 2713 (Wicks, 2026) proposed further amendments on provenance data that users download from platforms.
