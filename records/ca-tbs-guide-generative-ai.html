Summary

Guidance for federal institutions on generative AI risks (inaccuracy/hallucination, bias, privacy and security, IP, procedural fairness, over-reliance, undisclosed AI interactions, environmental costs) organised around the 'FASTER' principles (fair, accountable, secure, transparent, educated, relevant). It asks institutions to label AI-generated content, tell users when they interact with AI, test deployed tools for bias and vulnerabilities, including red teaming, plan independent audits, and route administrative-decision uses through the Directive on Automated Decision-Making.

Status notes

First released on 6 September 2023. The second version, of 20 February 2024, added enhanced definitions of the FASTER principles, sections on the responsibilities of federal institutions and on documentation requirements, and new frequently asked questions. A concise summary for public servants, Generative AI in Your Daily Work, followed on 15 October 2024. The guide says it will be updated regularly; its page was last modified on 24 September 2026. The Guide on the Use of Agentic Artificial Intelligence, published on 22 May 2026, complements it for agentic AI systems.
