Summary

Places a four-year moratorium on manufacturing, selling or offering for sale in California any toy that includes a companion chatbot, to give lawmakers time to modernise safety rules before such products reach children. It builds on the state's companion chatbot law, SB 243 (2025), whose civil action for injured persons applies to violations. It responds to findings that AI toys discussed sexually explicit topics in depth, told children where to find dangerous objects such as matches or knives, used addictive features to increase use, and posed data privacy risks.
