Summary

EU-style risk-based AI bill: unacceptable-risk systems (e.g., subliminal manipulation, categorisation using sensitive data, indiscriminate facial recognition) are banned; high-risk systems need risk management, technical documentation, human oversight, cybersecurity and post-deployment monitoring; limited-risk systems carry transparency duties; includes incident reporting.

Status notes

Executive bill (message) filed in the Chamber of Deputies on 7 May 2024 and merged on 29 May 2024 with motion 15869-19, which the Senate lists as the lead bulletin. The Chamber approved it in general on 4 Aug 2025 and in particular on 13 Oct 2025 and sent it to the Senate the same day; on 14 Oct 2025 the Senate referred it to its Committee on Future Challenges, Science, Technology and Innovation, which was still in the general study at its session of 13 Jul 2026. On 26 May 2026 the Minister of Science, Ximena Lincolao, gave the committee a proposal for a different framework built on six pillars, and the committee chair, Sen. Rojo Edwards, expected a substitute amendment shifting from review before marketing to review after it. The Senate's record shows no amendment filed up to its last entry, a renewal of simple urgency on 1 Sep 2026.
