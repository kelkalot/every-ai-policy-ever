Summary

Updated national AI policy to 2031 with a strengthened governance and ethics axis: human-centred principles (human oversight, robustness and security, transparency, accountability), a call for a comprehensive rights-respecting AI regulatory framework, ethical and transparency requirements in public procurement, and attention to children, disinformation and cybersecurity.

Scope

Borderline scope A national AI policy with three axes (enabling factors, development and adoption, and governance and ethics); included as Chile's national AI policy, whose 2024 update centres on the governance and ethics axis.

Status notes

Approved by Decree No. 12 of 11 Apr 2024 and published in the Diario Oficial (No. 44,060) on 28 Jan 2025; Article II of the decree repeals Supreme Decree No. 20 of 2021, which approved the first policy. The update keeps the policy's ten-year horizon, so it applies until 2031, and centres on its third axis, governance and ethics, adding sub-axes on international articulation, environment and climate crisis, inclusion and non-discrimination, children and adolescents, and culture and heritage. The Ministry presented it on 2 May 2024 with an action plan of 177 measures, 100 of them due by 2026, whose progress 14 ministries follow every six months.
