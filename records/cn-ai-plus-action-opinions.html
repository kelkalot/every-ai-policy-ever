Summary

State Council opinions on the 'AI+' action, mainly about integrating AI into six fields: science and technology, industry, consumption, people's livelihood, governance and global cooperation, with targets for 2027, 2030 and 2035. Its item on raising security capability calls for security capacity for models and algorithms, data, infrastructure and applications; guarding against risks from model black boxes, hallucination and algorithmic discrimination; forward-looking assessment; and systems for AI technology monitoring, risk early warning and emergency response. It also calls for improving AI laws and ethical norms and optimising security assessment and filing.

Language

Scope

Borderline scope A State Council policy on integrating AI across the economy and society in which safety takes up a small part of the text, mainly item (14) on security capability and two sentences of item (13) on law, ethics and security assessment; included as the State Council's national AI policy.

Status notes

Signed 21 Aug 2025 and released on 26 Aug 2025. The NDRC coordinates implementation. Item (14) also calls for an inclusive and prudent, categorised and graded approach to AI governance. Outside items (13) and (14), item (6)2 calls for jointly assessing and responding to AI application risks to keep AI development safe, reliable and controllable, and item (4)1 for assessing the employment risks of AI applications.
