Summary

Version 1.0 of the AI Safety Governance Framework, issued by TC260 under the Global AI Governance Initiative. Its principles: be inclusive and prudent to ensure safety; identify risks with agile governance; integrate technology and management for coordinated response; promote openness and cooperation. It classifies inherent safety risks (models and algorithms, data, systems) and application safety risks in the cyber, real-world, cognitive and ethical domains, including misuse for nuclear, biological and chemical weapons and uncontrollable AI. It sets technical and governance measures, such as tiered management and incident response, and safety guidelines for developers, providers and users.
