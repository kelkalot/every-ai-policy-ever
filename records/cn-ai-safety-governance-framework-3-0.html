AI Safety Governance Framework 3.0

Summary

Version 3.0 of China's AI safety governance framework. It keeps the logic of risk classification, technical response and comprehensive governance, with inherent, application and derivative risks, up to AI escaping human control. It describes unintended autonomous model behaviour, such as refusing shutdown instructions or strategic underperformance in evaluations, and adds sandbox supervision. Annexes set five-level risk-grading principles whose top level has catastrophic and systemic features, a life-cycle risk management framework for AI agents, and basic principles for trustworthy AI such as ultimate human control. It calls for research on consensus guidelines for catastrophic AI risks.

Status notes

Released on 14 Sept 2026 at the opening ceremony of the 2026 National Cybersecurity Publicity Week. TC260 prepared it under the guidance of the Cyberspace Administration of China, with the China Academy of Cyberspace Studies, the CAC Data and Technology Support Center and other specialist institutions, research institutes and companies. It builds on versions 1.0 (2024) and 2.0 (2025) and replaces version 2.0. Compared with version 2.0 it adds the annex on AI agents and the section on sandbox supervision, which includes exploring liability exemptions for faultless conduct in sandbox tests; the risk-grading and trustworthy-AI annexes carry over, the latter extended to respect for digital sovereignty and international mutual recognition of model evaluations.
