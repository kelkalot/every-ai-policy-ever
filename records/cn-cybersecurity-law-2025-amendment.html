Summary

Decision of the NPC Standing Committee making 14 changes to the Cybersecurity Law. It adds an article on AI, Article 20 of the amended law: the state supports basic AI theory research and R&D of key technologies such as algorithms, builds training-data resources, computing power and other infrastructure, improves AI ethics norms, strengthens risk monitoring, assessment and safety supervision, and promotes AI applications and healthy development; it also supports using AI and other new technologies to improve cybersecurity protection. Other changes raise the penalties for network operators and critical information infrastructure operators.

Scope

Borderline scope Not AI-specific: an amendment to the Cybersecurity Law, included for the article on AI research, ethics norms, risk monitoring and assessment and safety supervision that it adds (Art. 20 of the amended law).

Status notes

Adopted on 28 Oct 2025 at the 18th session of the 14th NPC Standing Committee and in force from 1 Jan 2026. The Cybersecurity Law is republished with the changes and its articles renumbered. Besides the AI article, the Decision adds an article on Party leadership of cybersecurity work and the overall national security concept (new Art. 3), requires network operators handling personal information to comply with the Civil Code and the Personal Information Protection Law, raises fines up to 10 million yuan for especially serious consequences, adds penalties for selling uncertified critical network equipment and dedicated security products, and allows lighter or no penalties in the cases the Administrative Penalty Law provides.
