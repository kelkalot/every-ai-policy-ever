Summary

Regulates internet information services that use 'deep synthesis' technology, defined as the use of deep learning, virtual reality and other generative or synthetic algorithms to produce text, images, audio, video, virtual scenes and other online information (25 articles). Providers must verify users' real identities, review users' inputs and synthesised outputs, and conspicuously label content that may cause public confusion or misidentification. Providers and technical supporters must run security assessments of tools that generate or edit biometric information such as faces and voices. Providers with public-opinion attributes or social-mobilisation capacity must file their algorithms.

Status notes

Adopted at the CAC office meeting of 3 Nov 2022, signed 25 Nov 2022 and published 11 Dec 2022. Technical supporters must also complete algorithm filing by reference to the provider rules (Art. 19). Its labelling articles (Arts. 16 and 17) are built on by the 2025 Measures for Labeling AI-Generated and Synthetic Content (CN-09), which, with the mandatory standard GB 45438-2025 (CN-08), set more detailed labelling duties from 1 Sept 2025.
