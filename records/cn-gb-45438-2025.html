Summary

Mandatory national standard that sets the technical methods for explicit labels and implicit (metadata) labels on AI-generated and synthetic content. It is the technical companion to the AI Content Labeling Measures.

Language

English translation by Standardization Administration of China national standards platform (English edition prepared under TC260; translated by the China Electronics Standardization Institute and others) Open it. The Chinese text is authoritative.

Status notes

Published 28 Feb 2025 and effective 1 Sept 2025, the same day as the Measures for Labeling AI-Generated and Synthetic Content (CN-09), whose Art. 11 requires labelling to also meet mandatory national standards. It is a mandatory 'GB' standard, not a recommended 'GB/T' one. The Chinese title covers content generated and synthesised by AI (人工智能生成合成内容); the official English title shortens this to 'content generated by artificial intelligence'. An English-language edition, translated under TC260, was published on 30 Jan 2026.
