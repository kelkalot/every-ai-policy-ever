Summary

China's action plan for international AI governance, in 13 items. On safety it calls for timely AI risk assessment with targeted prevention and response measures, towards a widely recognised safety governance framework; a risk testing and evaluation system; and mechanisms for threat information sharing and emergency response. It proposes exploring categorised and tiered management and traceability management systems for AI services. It also calls for regular safety assessment of AI used in public services, technical safety guidelines for open-source communities, and support for the UN Independent International Scientific Panel on AI and the Global Dialogue on AI Governance.

Status notes

Released 26 Jul 2025 at the 2025 World Artificial Intelligence Conference (WAIC) and High-Level Meeting on Global AI Governance. The text does not mention the World AI Cooperation Organization, which China proposed at the same conference and which was set up by treaty in July 2026 (see INT-15).
