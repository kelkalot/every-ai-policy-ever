Summary

China's statement of its position on global AI governance. It calls for a testing and assessment system based on AI risk levels, tiered and category-based management, keeping AI under human control, explainability and traceability, a prudent and responsible approach to military AI, and discussions under the UN on an international institution to govern AI.
