Summary

TC260 technical document setting the basic security requirements that generative AI service providers must follow, in support of the Interim Measures for generative AI services. It covers corpus security (sources with over 5% illegal and unhealthy information are excluded), model security, security measures, keyword libraries and test question banks, and the security assessment that providers report on when they complete filing procedures. Appendix A lists 31 main security risks of corpora and generated content. Providers must also watch long-term risks such as AI able to deceive humans, self-replicate or self-modify, and its use for malware or biological or chemical weapons.
