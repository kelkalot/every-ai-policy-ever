Summary

Bill to regulate AI in Colombia and guarantee its ethical and responsible development. The text proposed for first debate bans social scoring by public authorities, real-time remote biometric identification for mass surveillance without judicial authorisation, harmful subliminal manipulation, biometric inference of sensitive traits, and systems that attack targets without a prior human decision. High-impact systems need risk management, impact assessment, human oversight and serious-incident reporting; chatbots must disclose themselves and deepfakes be labelled. It adds rights to explanation and human review, child protection, an Intersectoral AI Commission, sandboxes and fines.

Status notes

Filed in the House of Representatives on 21 Jul 2026 by members of the House and the Senate and published in Gaceta del Congreso No. 878 of 2026. It takes up the bill that the Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation and the Ministry of ICT presented in the 2022-2026 term, PL 324/2025 Cámara – 043/2025 Senado, which was archived under Article 190 of Law 5 of 1992. The House's Sixth Committee designated rapporteurs on 29 Aug 2026 (one replaced on 3 Sep 2026) and held a technical roundtable on 15 Sep 2026. The first-debate report, posted in Sep 2026, proposes a rewritten text: it adds articles on synthetic-content labelling, the right to explanation and human review, and child protection, drops the filed text's National Advisory Council of AI Experts and makes a National AI Committee advisory to the Intersectoral AI Commission (the Ministry of Science, the Ministry of ICT and the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce acting jointly, without a new entity). Under the proposed Article 34 the prohibitions would apply from promulgation, the high-impact obligations 12 months later unless the Commission or the sectoral authority sets another period, and the other provisions later still (the text reads 'veinticuatro (18) meses', giving two different figures). Fines for the most serious infringements reach 3,000 monthly minimum wages or 150% of the economic benefit obtained.
