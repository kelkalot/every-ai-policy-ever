Summary

Plan for applying the EU AI Act in Czechia, approved by the Government on 28 May 2025. The Ministry of Industry and Trade coordinates implementation, prepares a national AI law and sets up an AI competence centre for eGovernment. The Czech Telecommunication Office is the market surveillance authority; the Office for Technical Standardisation, Metrology and State Testing (ÚNMZ) is the notifying authority, which designates and monitors the bodies that assess the conformity of high-risk AI systems; and the Czech Standardisation Agency builds and runs a regulatory sandbox in which companies and researchers test AI under regulators' supervision.

Status notes

At the same meeting the Government created the post of Government Commissioner for Artificial Intelligence and appointed Jan Kavalírek, Deputy Minister of Industry and Trade. The Czech Standardisation Agency signed a memorandum of cooperation with the Czech Artificial Intelligence Association on the regulatory sandbox. The draft national AI law that follows from the plan is CZ-01.
