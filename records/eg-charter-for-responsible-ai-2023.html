Summary

Egypt's first national interpretation of international guidance on ethical and responsible AI, issued by the National Council for AI as version 1.0 in 2023. It has 13 general guidelines for all members of the AI ecosystem and 16 implementation guidelines for entities developing, deploying or managing AI, each tagged with one of five principles. They include the right to know when one is interacting with an AI system, final human determination, systematic AI risk management across the development life cycle, impact assessments before all government AI projects and MCIT supervision of them, and testing of foreign AI products before they enter the Egyptian market.

Status notes

Labelled version 1.0 (2023). The five principles are human-centeredness, transparency and explainability, fairness, accountability, and security and safety. The charter is to be reviewed annually, with a public consultation before each revision, and draws on guidelines of the OECD, UNESCO, WHO, IEEE and the EU and of several countries. The 2026 Guide to Egypt's National AI Governance Framework (EG-02) operationalises its principles, and the second National AI Strategy (EG-04) plans detailed guidelines for it.
