Summary

Second edition of Egypt's national AI strategy for 2025-2030, issued by the National Council for AI and built on six pillars: governance, technology, data, ICT and AI infrastructure, ecosystem and talents. The governance pillar serves the objective 'Ensure Ethical and Responsible AI Use'. Its 'Egypt National AI Regulatory System' initiative plans a Center for Responsible AI, guidelines and standards for classifying AI system risks, detailed guidelines for the Egyptian Charter for Responsible AI, regulations for AI testing and deployment, an AI assurance framework, and an AI law with bylaws and a regulatory body to protect citizens against high-risk or prohibited AI.

Scope

Borderline scope Mainly an AI adoption and economic development strategy; included for its governance pillar, which commits to an AI regulatory system, an AI law and rules for safe AI testing and deployment.

Status notes

The strategy opens with a statement by the President of the Republic launching the second edition, which builds on the first National AI Strategy launched in May 2021. A second governance initiative covers global and regional AI dialogues, including unified Arab AI standards. Most of the strategy concerns economic and capacity goals for 2030 across 21 initiatives, such as an ICT contribution of 7.7% to GDP, 30,000 AI professionals and more than 250 successful AI companies.
