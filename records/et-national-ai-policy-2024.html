Summary

National AI policy dated June 2024, developed under the leadership of the Ethiopian Artificial Intelligence Institute and taking effect on Council of Ministers approval. Besides skills, infrastructure, data and research, it sets out AI risks (data privacy and security, ethics and bias, economic, social and cultural impact) with remedies, and commits to a legal and ethical framework: AI ethical guidelines on fairness, accountability and transparency, regular examination of AI applications, proclamations on AI development and on AI data, products, licensing and inspection, certification of domestic and imported AI products and services, and regulating the pace and extent of AI deployment.

Scope

Borderline scope Mainly a policy for AI skills, infrastructure, data, research and adoption; included as Ethiopia's national AI policy, which sets out AI risks and remedies and commits to ethical guidelines, licensing, certification and AI proclamations.

Status notes

The bilingual document (Amharic and English) is dated June 2024 (Sene 2016 in the Ethiopian calendar). It takes effect on approval by the Council of Ministers in line with Council of Ministers Regulation No. 510/2022 and applies to AI initiatives at federal, regional and city administration levels. Digital Policy Alert dates the Council of Ministers' approval to its 37th regular meeting on 27 June 2024. Once the policy is adopted, the Ethiopian Artificial Intelligence Institute coordinates, monitors and evaluates its implementation, and the policy foresees proclamations, regulations and directives on AI development, data, imported and domestic AI products and services, licensing and inspection.
