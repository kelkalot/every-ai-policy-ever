Summary

Establishes the European Artificial Intelligence Office within the Commission's Directorate-General for Communication Networks, Content and Technology to implement and enforce the AI Act. Its tasks include developing tools, methodologies and benchmarks to evaluate general-purpose AI models, in particular very large models with systemic risks; monitoring unforeseen risks from such models, including alerts from the scientific panel; investigating possible infringements; supporting the rules on prohibited practices and high-risk systems; providing the AI Board's secretariat and facilitating codes of practice; and contributing to international cooperation on AI.

Status notes

Adopted on 24 January 2024 and published in the Official Journal C series on 14 February 2024; it entered into force on 21 February 2024 (Article 9). It was adopted ahead of the AI Act, after the co-legislators' political agreement of 8 December 2023, so that preparation of the Act's implementation could start, and it says it may be revised once the Regulation is adopted. The Office's officials come from the Directorate-General's staff and its operational expenditure from the Digital Europe Programme's specific objective on AI. It is not a European Office within the meaning of the Financial Regulation. As of September 2026 the Office has more than 125 staff in six units, among them unit A3 'AI Safety', and two advisers, a Lead Scientific Adviser and an International Affairs Adviser. Under the powers the AI Act gives the Commission it enforces the rules for general-purpose AI models: it can evaluate models, request information and measures from providers and, in case of infringement, restrict a model's public availability and issue direct fines.
