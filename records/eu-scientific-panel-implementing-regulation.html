Summary

Establishes, under Article 68(1) of the AI Act, a scientific panel of independent experts in AI to support enforcement and advise the AI Office. The panel has at most 60 experts, appointed for two-year terms after a public call, with one to three nationals of each EU and EEA-EFTA state where qualified applicants exist; they must be independent of providers of AI systems and general-purpose AI models, declare their interests and work transparently. Market surveillance authorities can request the panel's support, and the Regulation sets the procedures for the panel to issue qualified alerts to the AI Office and to request information from providers of general-purpose AI models.

Status notes

Adopted on 7 March 2025 (Commission document C/2025/1377) and published in the Official Journal (OJ L, 2025/454) on 10 March 2025; it entered into force on 30 March 2025, the twentieth day after publication. The AI Office and the Joint Research Centre jointly provide the panel's secretariat. On 1 June 2026 the Commission appointed the Scientific Panel, with 60 independent experts, and the Advisory Forum, for two-year terms; the Panel focuses on general-purpose AI models and systems, systemic risks, model classification, evaluation methodologies and cross-border market surveillance. On 9 October 2026 the Commission held a special meeting of the Scientific Panel on frontier AI safety and risks, at which the panel was to present recommendations on frontier AI safety and security risks; the panel had been investigating recent loss-of-control incidents and, with the AI Office, had worked on a set of questions for the companies that developed the models involved.
