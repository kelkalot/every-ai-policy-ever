Summary

Supreme Court of Florida opinion amending Chapter 4, the Rules of Professional Conduct, of the Rules Regulating The Florida Bar on the Bar's petition. It adds generative artificial intelligence to the comments on four rules: competence includes understanding the benefits and risks of technology, including generative AI; lawyers should be aware that generative AI may create risks to the duty of confidentiality; firms' internal policies should consider safeguards for their use of generative AI; and lawyers should consider safeguards when assistants or outside services use it. The amendments also allow testimonials by celebrity clients in lawyer advertising.

Status notes

The Florida Bar's Board of Governors approved the proposed amendments and published them for comment in The Florida Bar News; no comments were received. The Court adopted them except the proposal to delete 'zeal' and 'zealous' from the Preamble and the comment to rule 4-1.3, and the proposed amendments to rule 4-8.6 on suspended individuals and authorized business entities. The generative-AI language is in the Comments to rules 4-1.1, 4-1.6, 4-5.1 and 4-5.3, not in the text of the rules. The amendments took effect on 28 Oct 2024; a motion for rehearing would not alter that date.
