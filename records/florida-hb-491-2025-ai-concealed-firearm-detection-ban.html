Summary

Bill that would have prohibited any state, county, local or municipal governmental agency from using artificial intelligence, or contracting with another entity to use it, together with a camera, video recording, live-streaming device or similar technology to detect concealed firearms in a public place. Agencies could still use such AI on school grounds and at the locations where concealed weapons licensees may not carry. An aggrieved party could seek injunctive relief against the agency or its contractor to prevent or remedy a violation.

Status notes

Filed 10 Feb 2025. The Criminal Justice Subcommittee reported it favourably with a committee substitute (13-0) on 26 Mar 2025, narrowing the filed version, which applied to any person and carried a criminal penalty, to governmental agencies and replacing the penalty with injunctive relief. The Judiciary Committee reported it favourably (19-0) on 17 Apr 2025, and the House passed it 114-1 on 24 Apr 2025. In the Senate it was referred to Rules, indefinitely postponed and withdrawn from consideration on 3 May 2025, and died in Rules on 16 Jun 2025. The similar Senate bill SB 562 (Ingoglia) died in the Criminal Justice Committee. The stated effective date was 1 Jul 2025.
