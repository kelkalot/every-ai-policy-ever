Summary

France's national institute for AI evaluation and security, created on 31 January 2025 ahead of the Paris AI Action Summit and steered by SGDSN and the Directorate General for Enterprise (DGE). It brings together ANSSI, Inria, LNE and PEReN to build a public capacity to evaluate advanced AI. Its 2026-2027 roadmap sets eleven projects: evaluation tools for AI Act market surveillance authorities, standards work and synthetic-content detection; research on systemic risks from information manipulation, offensive cyber capabilities and CBRN proliferation, and on agentic systems; and performance challenges. SGDSN represents France in the international network of AI safety institutes.

Status notes

SGDSN announced on 12 Feb 2026 that INESIA had adopted its 2026-2027 roadmap, with eleven projects in three poles (support for AI regulation, systemic risks, performance and reliability) and a cross-cutting axis. SGDSN says the network of AI Safety Institutes was renamed the 'International Network for Advanced AI Measurement, Evaluation and Science' in December 2025. The DGE leads exchanges with the European Commission, in particular its AI Office.
