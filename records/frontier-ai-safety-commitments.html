Summary

Leading AI developers commit to assess frontier-model risks across the lifecycle and to set and monitor thresholds for intolerable severe risks. They agree not to develop or deploy a model if those risks cannot be kept below the thresholds, and to maintain internal accountability. They also commit to publish a safety framework and report publicly on implementation, with deeper information shared with trusted actors such as home governments.

Status notes

The UK and Republic of Korea governments announced the first 16 signatories: Amazon, Anthropic, Cohere, Google, G42, IBM, Inflection AI, Meta, Microsoft, Mistral AI, Naver, OpenAI, Samsung Electronics, Technology Innovation Institute, xAI and Zhipu.ai. Magic, Minimax, 01.ai and NVIDIA were added on 7 February 2025, making 20. Signatories undertook to publish a safety framework focused on severe risks by the next AI summit, in France (the AI Action Summit, INT-01), and to provide public updates if their approaches to the three outcomes change.
