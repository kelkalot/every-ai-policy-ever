Summary

Leaders' declaration of the first G20 summit in Africa. Its section on 'Artificial Intelligence, Data Governance and Innovation for Sustainable Development' (paragraphs 44-47) welcomes the G20 AI task force, reaffirms the G20 AI Principles and commits to cooperation to share AI's benefits and mitigate risks. It states that safe, secure and trustworthy AI requires addressing human rights, transparency and explainability, fairness, accountability, regulation, safety, appropriate human oversight, ethics, bias, privacy, data protection and data governance, and recognises the UN's role in AI cooperation. Leaders also welcome the AI for Africa Initiative.

Scope

Borderline scope A general G20 summit declaration whose AI content is limited to paragraphs 44-47; included for its leaders' commitment on safe, secure and trustworthy AI and its reaffirmation of the G20 AI Principles.

Status notes

Adopted at the very start of the summit on 22 November 2025, in the absence of the United States; President Ramaphosa announced the adoption at the leaders' reception that evening. The declaration has 122 paragraphs; its AI section is paragraphs 44-47 under the heading 'Artificial Intelligence, Data Governance and Innovation for Sustainable Development'. Paragraph 46 notes the Technology Policy Assistance Facility set up by UNESCO under the South African Presidency to support countries in shaping their AI policies. Paragraph 122 commits members to working together under the United States' Presidency in 2026 and to meeting in the United Kingdom in 2027 and the Republic of Korea in 2028.
