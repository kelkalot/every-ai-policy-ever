Summary

Commitment by the G7 leaders at their 2018 Charlevoix summit to promote human-centric AI and its commercial adoption. They commit to safeguard privacy, invest in cybersecurity, promote industry research on safety, assurance, data quality and data security, encourage AI that addresses accountability, assurance, liability, security, safety, bias and potential misuse, foster initiatives that promote safety and transparency and give guidance on human intervention in AI decision-making, and improve digital security in AI through voluntary codes of conduct, standards or guidelines, alongside commitments on skills, gender equality and adoption by small firms.

Status notes

One of eight declarations the G7 leaders issued on 9 June 2018 at the 44th G7 summit in La Malbaie (Charlevoix), Quebec. It refers to the 2018 G7 Montreal Ministerial Statement on Artificial Intelligence and to the multi-stakeholder, human-centric vision of the 2017 G7 ICT and Industry Ministers' Torino Declaration. Its other commitments cover lifelong learning and reskilling, the participation of women and underrepresented groups, multistakeholder dialogue, support for unpaid caregivers, and an open and fair market environment including the free flow of information. Global Affairs Canada published it in English and in French (Vision commune de Charlevoix sur l'avenir de l'intelligence artificielle).
