Summary

G7 Leaders' statement committing to drive innovation and adoption of 'secure, responsible, and trustworthy AI' that benefits people, mitigates negative externalities and promotes national security. It covers public-sector AI adoption that respects human rights and privacy and promotes transparency, fairness and accountability (a G7 GovAI Grand Challenge and a G7 AI Network), the 2024 G7 action plan on safe, secure and trustworthy AI in the world of work, a workplan on AI and energy, and partnerships with developing countries. An annexed G7 AI Adoption Roadmap for SMEs includes trust-building tools based on the Hiroshima AI Process and its OECD-run reporting framework.

Status notes

Issued on 17 June 2025 at the Kananaskis summit, Alberta, under the Canadian G7 Presidency. The statement tasks ministers with a workplan on AI and energy before the end of 2025; the annexed G7 AI Adoption Roadmap commits to an AI Adoption Blueprint prepared with the OECD, a toolkit for AI deployers and raising awareness of the HAIP Code of Conduct Reporting Framework. The G7 Industry, Digital and Technology Ministerial Declaration of 9 December 2025 (Montréal) reports on delivering the Roadmap, including the SME AI Adoption Blueprint launched by the Canadian Presidency, the Toolkit for SMEs Deploying Artificial Intelligence and the G7 AI Network of public-sector AI leaders, and the ministers' statement on the Blueprint of the same date responds to this statement. The French presidency continued the work in the May 2026 ministerial declaration (G7-04).
