Summary

Establishes consumer protections for AI companions. Operators must disclose that users are interacting with AI, provide crisis resources, adopt response protocols for user prompts about suicidal ideation or self-harm and report annually to the Behavioral Health Administration of the Department of Health. The Act sets guidelines against addictive software patterns aimed at minors and against chatbots presenting themselves as human; AI companions may not pose as licensed mental health professionals or encourage harmful behaviour, and violations are subject to Hawaii's consumer protection laws.
