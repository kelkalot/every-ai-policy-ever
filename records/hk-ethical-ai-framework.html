Summary

Framework for government bureaux and departments, also offered in adapted form to other organisations, on building ethics into AI and big-data projects. It sets 12 Ethical AI Principles (including transparency and explainability, reliability, robustness and security, human oversight, safety and accountability) and an AI Application Impact Assessment. High-risk projects need IT Board or CIO review, and assessments are repeated at four life-cycle stages.

Status notes

First introduced in 2021 and updated in 2023, according to the Secretary for Innovation, Technology and Industry (30 Oct 2025). Current text is version 2.0 (December 2025), which updated Sections 1 to 5 and Appendices A to D and removed Appendix E. Earlier versions: 1.1 (June 2023), 1.2 (July 2023), 1.3 (August 2023) and 1.4 (July 2024, which replaced 'Office of the Government Chief Information Officer' with 'Digital Policy Office'). The framework was first developed for government bureaux and departments; this version is adapted for general reference by other organisations. Where the Chinese and English versions differ, the English version prevails.
