Summary

Non-binding guidance for generative AI developers, service providers and users on risks such as hallucination, bias, deepfakes, jailbreaking and data leakage. It sets a governance framework with five dimensions (privacy, IP, crime prevention, reliability, system security) and a four-tier risk model (unacceptable, high, limited, low). It assigns role-based duties, including content labelling and watermarking of high-risk outputs, independent audits, pre-deployment risk assessment and human oversight matched to impact.

Status notes

Released by the Digital Policy Office on 15 Apr 2025; the current text is version 1.1 (December 2025). The DPO commissioned the Hong Kong Generative AI Research and Development Center (HKGAI), established in 2023 with AIR@InnoHK funding, to study and suggest codes and guidelines, and promulgated the guideline after considering its findings; it says it will update the guideline regularly. Where the Chinese and English versions differ, the English version prevails. It is published on the same DPO page as the Ethical AI Framework (HK-01).
