Summary

Guidelines by the High-Level Expert Group on AI set up by the European Commission on what makes AI trustworthy: lawful, respecting all applicable laws; ethical, respecting ethical principles and values; and robust, technically and with regard to its social environment. They set seven key requirements: human agency and oversight; technical robustness and safety, including a fall back plan; privacy and data governance; transparency, including that people know they are interacting with an AI system; diversity, non-discrimination and fairness; societal and environmental well-being; and accountability, with auditability and redress. An assessment list helps verify that they are met.

Status notes

Written by the High-Level Expert Group on Artificial Intelligence (AI HLEG), an independent expert group that the European Commission set up in June 2018, and presented on 8 April 2019, after a first draft published in December 2018 drew more than 500 comments in an open consultation. The assessment list in the guidelines was piloted by the Commission from 26 June to 1 December 2019; on that basis the AI HLEG presented the final Assessment List for Trustworthy AI (ALTAI), a self-assessment checklist and the group's third deliverable, in July 2020 (made public on 17 July 2020). The guidelines are offered in 23 EU languages, together with a separate AI HLEG document on the definition of AI used for their purpose.
