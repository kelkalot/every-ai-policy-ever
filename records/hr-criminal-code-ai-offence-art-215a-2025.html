Act on Amendments to the Criminal Code (new Article 215.a: endangering life and property by an artificial intelligence system)

Zakon o izmjenama i dopunama Kaznenog zakona

Summary

Amendment to Croatia's Criminal Code, adopted on 24 October 2025, that adds the AI Act's definition of an AI system (Article 87(33)) and a new offence, Article 215.a 'Endangering life and property by an artificial intelligence system'. Whoever, in the development, testing, verification, supervision, management or use of an AI system, or otherwise by means of one, causes danger to human life or body or to large-scale property faces six months to five years' imprisonment, or up to three years if negligent. Serious bodily injury or large-scale damage raises this to one to ten years (six months to five if negligent), death to three to fifteen years (one to eight if negligent).

Scope

Borderline scope Amendment of the Criminal Code that is not AI-specific as a whole, but adds the AI-system definition and the offence in Article 215.a; included because it is the operative criminal provision on AI-caused danger to life and property.

Status notes

The Minister of Justice, Public Administration and Digital Transformation, Damir Habijan, presented the bill at its first reading in the Croatian Parliament in September 2025, together with amendments to the Criminal Procedure Act and the act on the anti-corruption office (USKOK); he said the definition of an AI system was taken from the AI Act and that a separate artificial intelligence act had to be adopted by 2026. Proclaimed by the President of the Republic on 30 October 2025 and published in Narodne novine No. 136/2025 on 5 November 2025; under its Article 14 it entered into force on the eighth day after publication, 13 November 2025. Article 215.a applies only where no more serious criminal offence is committed. The same act also extends Croatian criminal law to offences of legal persons committed in Croatia, on Croatian ships and aircraft and abroad, rewrites the offence of trafficking in human beings (Article 106), adds Directive (EU) 2024/1712 to the list of transposed directives and amends the bribery offences concerning members of representative bodies (Article 339).
