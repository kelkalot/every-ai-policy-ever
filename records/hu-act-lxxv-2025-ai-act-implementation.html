Summary

Hungary's framework law for applying the EU AI Act. It provides for a government-designated AI notifying authority, which assesses, designates, notifies and monitors conformity assessment bodies for high-risk systems, and an AI market surveillance authority with general competence that also runs the national AI regulatory sandbox and acts as the single point of contact. The market surveillance authority may impose administrative fines, with amounts set by government decree. The Act creates the Hungarian Artificial Intelligence Council, an advisory body without decision-making powers chaired by a Prime Minister's appointee, and calls for a national AI strategy.

Status notes

Adopted by the National Assembly on 21 October 2025 and promulgated on 31 October 2025. In force on the 31st day after promulgation (1 December 2025); section 3(2) and section 10 on the AI regulatory sandbox took effect on 2 August 2026. Government Decree 344/2025 (X. 31.) (HU-02) designates the authorities and sets the detailed rules on their procedures and on fines. Until the end of the term set in Government Resolution 1028/2025 (II. 24.), the Government Commissioner for AI chairs the AI Council. The Commission's list of single points of contact (last updated 7 September 2026) showed no entry for Hungary.
