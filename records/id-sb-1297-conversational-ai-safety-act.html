Summary

Requires operators of conversational AI chatbots to tell users the service is AI and to adopt protocols for responding to suicidal ideation or self-harm. For minor users (under 18), operators may not use addictive reward systems to encourage engagement, must prevent the chatbot from generating sexually explicit material, making sexual suggestions or sexually objectifying the minor, and must keep it from statements that could mislead minors into thinking it is human, including statements simulating emotional dependence or role-playing adult-minor relationships. Operators must provide tools for minors and parents to manage privacy settings, with special provisions for users under 13.
