Summary

Limits the use of artificial intelligence in therapy and psychotherapy services. It prohibits anyone from using AI to provide mental health care and therapeutic decision-making, while allowing licensed behavioural health professionals to use AI for administrative and supplementary support services. Its stated aims are to protect patients from unregulated and unqualified AI products and to protect children amid concerns over AI chatbot use in youth mental health services. The Department of Financial and Professional Regulation may investigate suspected violations, and each confirmed violation carries a fine of up to $10,000.

Status notes

Signed by Governor Pritzker on Friday 1 Aug 2025 and announced by the Department of Financial and Professional Regulation (IDFPR) on 4 Aug 2025; it took effect on signature. The Act passed both chambers of the General Assembly unanimously and was developed with IDFPR and the National Association of Social Workers, Illinois Chapter.
