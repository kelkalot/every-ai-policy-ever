Summary

Amends the Illinois Human Rights Act so that an employer's use of AI in recruitment, hiring, promotion, discharge, discipline or other employment terms that discriminates against protected classes (including use of zip codes as a proxy) is a civil rights violation. Employers must notify employees when they use AI for these purposes.
