Summary

Iceland's first national AI action plan, with 20 actions in five pillars (AI to benefit everyone, competitive business, education, public services, healthcare), building on the 2021 AI strategy. Its safety-governance content includes Action 2, which assesses the adaptations arising from incorporating the EU AI Act through the EEA Agreement and prepares an AI bill naming the responsible institutions (spring 2026); Action 5, which analyses AI's effect on democratic debate, information disorder and foreign interference and sets guidelines for transparent AI use in politics (autumn 2026); and Action 16, updating standards for safe, transparent public-sector use of AI (spring 2027).

Scope

Borderline scope National AI action plan whose main focus is benefit, business, education, public services and health; its safety-governance content is Action 2 (AI Act incorporation), Action 5 (democratic debate, information disorder) and Action 16 (safe public-sector use). Included with a scope note.

Status notes

Published in July 2025 in Icelandic as 'Aðgerðaáætlun um gervigreind 2025-2027' (ISBN 978-9935-570-03-1) and in English (ISBN 978-9935-570-05-5); the government announced the English edition on 1 July 2025 and the Icelandic one on 2 July 2025. The plan is valid for two years from publication, with a review every six months and a comprehensive review no later than mid-2027; Iceland's 2021 AI strategy is to be reviewed in parallel. It builds on that strategy and on a November 2024 report on the economic opportunities of AI in Iceland, and each action names a responsible ministry, an implementing body and a time frame. The EFTA's EEA-Lex database lists the AI Act as under scrutiny for incorporation into the EEA Agreement by Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway (as stored on 17 October 2026).
