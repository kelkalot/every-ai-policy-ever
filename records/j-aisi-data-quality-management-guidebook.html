Summary

Guidebook of the Japan AI Safety Institute on managing the quality of data used to build and operate AI systems, on the premise that poor data quality degrades AI performance and trust. It treats data quality as one of the evaluation perspectives on AI safety, linked to fairness, robustness and verifiability, and organises international standards such as ISO/IEC 25012 and ISO/IEC 5259 into a framework of three views: a process view of the data life cycle from design to disposal, a governance cycle view, and a gateway view of data quality characteristics. It adds AI-specific points such as annotation quality, bias checks, drift management, synthetic data and monitoring.

Status notes

Version 1.00 was published on 31 Mar 2025, version 1.01 on 2 Dec 2025 and version 1.02 on 14 May 2026; version 1.02 turned the slide document into a text document and came with a machine-translated Japanese reference version and a data quality management checklist (version 1.00). The English text is the official edition. The guidebook was produced by J-AISI's standards team with data experts of IPA's Digital Infrastructure Center and J-AISI's Data Quality Sub-Working Group, and is maintained as a living document.
