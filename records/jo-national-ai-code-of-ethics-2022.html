Summary

Bilingual Arabic-English code of ethics prepared by the Ministry of Digital Economy and Entrepreneurship under Jordan's 2020 AI Policy and approved by the Council of Ministers, which circulated it to government bodies for compliance. It sets eight principles for the responsible use of AI (humanity and society; inclusiveness and justice; privacy and data; transparency; responsibility and accountability; reliability; integrity and non-fakeness; environment and sustainability), including human intervention in AI decisions, no use of AI to manipulate or mislead, testing before market release and accountability of natural persons for harm. It adds principles for the metaverse and AI research.

Status notes

The code cites as its legal basis Article 3(d) of the Telecommunications Law No. 13 of 1995, Article 61 of the General Policy for the Communications, Information Technology and Postal Sectors of 2018, and Article 21 of the Jordan Artificial Intelligence Policy 2020, which requires national regulatory frameworks for the responsible use of AI. The Ministry prepared it through a technical committee of experts from the public and private sectors, civil society and academia. It addresses individuals, public and private institutions, international and local organisations and civil society, inside or outside Jordan, and recommends complaint channels at the Ministry for AI-ethics complaints. It also lists the risks of not adhering to it. The Arabic title translates literally as 'National Charter for Artificial Intelligence Ethics'.
