Summary

Framework law for promoting the research, development and utilization of AI-related technology. Its basic principles require transparency in research, development and utilization, since wrongful or inappropriate use can abet crime, leaks of personal information and copyright infringement, and seek a leading role in international cooperation. The State is to analyse cases in which such use infringed citizens' rights and interests and accordingly give guidance, advice and information to R&D institutions, utilization business operators and others; set guidelines in line with international norms; and adopt an AI Basic Plan. It creates the AI Strategic Headquarters, headed by the Prime Minister.

Status notes

Enacted 28 May 2025 and promulgated as Act No. 53 of 4 Jun 2025. Under Supplementary Provisions Article 1 it took effect on promulgation, except Chapters III and IV (AI Basic Plan and AI Strategic Headquarters), which took effect on 1 Sept 2025; Supplementary Provisions Article 2 requires the government to review the Act's enforcement. Article 7 obliges utilization business operators to cooperate with the measures of the State and local governments (協力しなければならない), while R&D institutions and citizens are to endeavour to cooperate (Articles 6 and 8); the Cabinet Office's provisional English overview renders the business duty as 'shall endeavor to cooperate'. The Act contains no penalty provisions. Both Houses attached supplementary resolutions. The AI Basic Plan (Dec 2025; second plan Jul 2026) and the Article 13 appropriateness guideline (Dec 2025) were adopted under it, and the second AI Basic Plan commits to continuously reviewing the Act.
