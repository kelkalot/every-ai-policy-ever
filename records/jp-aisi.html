Summary

Japan's AI Safety Institute (J-AISI), launched on 14 February 2024 with its secretariat in the Information-technology Promotion Agency (IPA) and designated by the Integrated Innovation Strategy 2024 as Japan's central institution for AI safety. It examines and promotes evaluation methods and standards for AI safety: it studies safety-related standards, check tools, countermeasures against disinformation, and AI and cybersecurity, examines AI test environments, supports the government with research and standard-setting, links industry, academia and national research institutes, and cooperates with counterpart bodies such as the UK and US AI Safety Institutes.

Status notes

Announced by Prime Minister Kishida at the AI Strategy Council on 21 December 2023 and launched on 14 February 2024. Its overview of 1 July 2026 describes it as a government-related organisation in which 12 ministries and agencies and 5 related organisations take part, with the secretariat in IPA, which falls under METI and the Digital Agency. Government policy on AI safety is discussed in a liaison council of the ministries whose secretariat is the Cabinet Office, and an AISI Steering Committee chaired by the Executive Director (Akiko Murakami, part-time) deliberates on its work. The second AI Basic Plan (14 Jul 2026) calls for strengthening AISI's technical evaluation and feeding its results into policy. AISI has no specific statutory basis in the AI Promotion Act.
