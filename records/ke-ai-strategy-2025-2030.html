Summary

Kenya's national AI strategy for 2025-2030, built on three pillars (digital infrastructure, data, and research and innovation) and four enablers, one of them governance. The governance goal plans a national AI and emerging technologies policy, local ethical and safety standards implemented through conformity assessment, safety audits and technical regulations, a national AI risk and safety institute, regulatory sandboxes and, as AI matures, an AI and Other Emerging Technologies Act. The ethics goal plans a mandatory ethical impact assessment for public-sector AI, accountable procurement guidelines and a complaints and redress mechanism.

Status notes

Dated March 2025; the ministry announced that it would launch the strategy on 27 March 2025 at the Kenyatta International Convention Center. Developed by the National AI Working Group and Steering Committee with support from Germany's Federal Ministry of Economic Cooperation and Development, the EU-funded Digital Transformation Center Kenya implemented by GIZ, Canada's IDRC and the UK's Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office. For each goal it sets objectives, flagship projects, outcomes and key performance indicators.
