Summary

Draft national policy for public comment adopting a risk-based, lifecycle and effects-based approach to AI and other emerging technologies used in or affecting Kenya. It proposes an AI and Other Emerging Technologies Governance Act, a statutory National Council as central regulator with binding guidelines, national risk classification, a registry of overseen systems, regulatory sandboxes and oversight of frontier-model testing; a Kenya AI Safety Institute for safety research, testing, red-teaming and incident analysis; an AI incident reporting framework; testing protocols for high-risk systems; procurement safeguards; human oversight; child safeguards; and human control for defence uses.

Status notes

Dated July 2026; the ministry's call for public comments set a submission deadline of 4 August 2026, with written submissions to the Committee on Artificial Intelligence and Other Emerging Technologies. It builds on the Kenya AI Strategy 2025-2030 (KE-01), the Digital Master Plan 2022-2032 and the Data Protection Act 2019, and underwent legal review, including by the Office of the Attorney General. For systems placed into service after the policy takes effect, full compliance would be required once the relevant implementing regulations come into force; systems already in operation would have a two-year transition, and high-risk legacy systems three years to comply fully.
