Summary

Draft guidance for operators subject to the safety duty of Article 32 of Korea's AI Basic Act, which under Article 24 of the Enforcement Decree covers AI systems meeting all three criteria: training compute of 10^26 floating-point operations or more; the most advanced (state-of-the-art) AI technology in current use; and a risk that may broadly and seriously affect life, physical safety and fundamental rights. It explains how to determine the systems in scope and who bears the duty, risk identification, assessment and mitigation across the life cycle, a risk-management system for safety incidents, and reporting, including submission of the safety measures to the Minister within three months.

Status notes

The NIA version (file dated 22 January 2026, 71 pages) opens with a notice that this draft guideline is still being written and may differ from the final version to be published. It explains, and reproduces in full, a draft MSIT notification on the methods of implementing the AI safety obligation (인공지능 안전성 확보 의무의 이행방법 등에 관한 고시(안)), whose number is left blank. The draft notification requires a first report to the Minister within 24 hours of recognising a safety incident, an initial-action report within 7 days and a result report within 15 days of the first report, and new submissions within one month of a substantial change that increases risk or of recognising a new risk. Cumulative compute is to be calculated from training records and system logs, including pre-processing and fine-tuning, by theoretical, empirical-statistical or hardware-based methods. The guideline is a reference document that neither replaces the law nor imposes new duties, though supervisors may use it when checking compliance.
