Summary

Guidance under Article 33(3) of Korea's AI Basic Act, which lets the Minister of Science and ICT establish and disseminate guidelines on the criteria and examples of 'high-impact AI'. It sets a two-step test: whether the AI system is used in an area listed in Article 2(4), such as energy supply, drinking water, health care, medical devices, nuclear safety, analysis of biometric information for criminal investigation or arrest, hiring and loan screening, transport, public-service decisions or student assessment; and whether that use may significantly affect or endanger life, physical safety or fundamental rights. Area-by-area decision flows and examples follow.

Status notes

Published on 22 January 2026, when the Act entered into force. NIA records revised uploads on 29 January 2026 (decision flows for criminal investigation and arrest), 13 February 2026 (health-care criteria and a non-applicable example) and 29 April 2026 (health-care decision flow); the file recorded here is the 29 April 2026 version (216 pages). Operators judge for themselves whether their AI is high-impact and may ask the Minister to confirm; the guideline says it is not legally binding, that the expert committee's advice on a request is non-binding and optional, and that the Minister's confirmation is an administrative view on which the courts have the final say. Annexes reproduce the Act (Act No. 20676), the draft Enforcement Decree and the request forms. MSIT's online form for confirmation requests asks operators to review the guideline first and refers them to an advice desk.
