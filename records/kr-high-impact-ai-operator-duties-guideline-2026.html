Guideline on the Responsibilities of High-Impact AI Business Operators (Article 34 of the AI Basic Act)

Summary

Guidance on the duties that Article 34 of Korea's AI Basic Act places on operators providing high-impact AI or products and services using it: a risk-management plan; a plan to explain, as far as technically feasible, the AI's final results, the main criteria used to derive them and an overview of the training data; a user-protection plan; human management and supervision of high-impact AI; and drafting and keeping documents that show the safety and reliability measures, plus other matters resolved by the committee named in the Act. It reproduces the decree and notification provisions and appends a document form, a self-check list and a hiring example.

Status notes

Published with the Act's entry into force on 22 January 2026 (111 pages). For each duty the guideline reproduces the corresponding article of the technical notification that sets out the detailed measures under Article 34(2): Articles 4 to 8 on the risk-management plan, the explanation plan, user protection, human management and supervision, and documents; its worked example cites the notification as '고영향 인공지능과 관련한 사업자의 책무 고시' without an official number. It explains decree Article 27: operators post the main elements of their risk-management, explanation and user-protection plans and the name and contact of the person supervising the AI on their premises or website, trade secrets excepted, and a using operator that takes over a system from a developer who has taken the measures under items 1 to 3, without significantly changing its functions, is deemed to have taken them. It refers to ISO/IEC TS 42119-2:2025 on AI system testing. The committee in Article 34(1)6 is named only as 'the Committee' in the guideline.
