Summary

Implementing act under Article 195 of Kyrgyzstan's Digital Code. It approves the requirements for the content of the declaration by which owners of 'increased-danger' AI systems confirm conformity with the Code's mandatory requirements (Annex 1) and the declaration form (Annex 2). All owners of AI systems used in Kyrgyzstan, whatever their legal form or ownership, must, before putting an increased-danger system into use, adopt the declaration in the approved form and publish it on their website as a digital document signed with a qualified digital signature and open to the public. The form identifies the owner, names the system and its purpose and states that it meets the Code's parameters.
