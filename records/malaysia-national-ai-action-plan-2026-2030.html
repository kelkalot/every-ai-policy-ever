Summary

Malaysia's five-year national AI plan (AI Nation 2030), issued by the Ministry of Digital with AI Malaysia (National AI Office) as secretariat. Its 'Trust via Responsible Governance' enablers propose a hybrid, risk-based framework with a central AI governance authority and a principles-based regulatory framework of definitions, risk tiers and obligations as the basis for future AI legislation; a national AI Trust Function to issue AI safety and security standards, oversee testing and certification, keep a public AI incident registry and lead the ASEAN AI Safety Network; board-level AI governance; and AI training before officials approve or oversee government AI systems.

Status notes

The Ministry of Digital's press statement of 28 Jul 2026 says the government introduced the plan that day, at the launch of AI Malaysia Berhad, which institutionalised the National AI Office set up on 12 Dec 2024. At the same launch it announced the Malaysia AI Safety Institute (Institut Keselamatan AI Malaysia), a role AI Malaysia is to carry out, and an AI Governance Bill (RUU Tadbir Urus AI) being drafted on a risk-based approach, open to public consultation and targeted for completion by the end of 2026. The plan is published in English and Malay and bears a 2026 copyright of the Ministry of Digital. Oversight lies with the National Digital Economy and 4IR Council (MED4IRN), chaired by the Prime Minister, with AI Malaysia as central secretariat. The plan builds on the National Guidelines on AI Governance and Ethics (AIGE), which it cites as published by MOSTI in 2023, and on the Data Sharing Act 2025 (Act 864). E14 proposes amendments to the Malaysian Code on Corporate Governance on board oversight and disclosure of AI.
