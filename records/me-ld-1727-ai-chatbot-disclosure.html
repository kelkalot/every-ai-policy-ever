Summary

Prohibits a person from using an artificial intelligence chatbot or any other computer technology to engage in trade and commerce with a consumer in a manner that may mislead or deceive a reasonable consumer into believing that the consumer is engaging with a human being, unless the consumer is notified in a clear and conspicuous manner that the consumer is not. An artificial intelligence chatbot is a software application, web interface or computer program that simulates human conversation and interaction through textual or aural communications. A violation is a violation of the Maine Unfair Trade Practices Act.

Status notes

Signed on 12 June 2025 as Public Law 2025, chapter 294, which adds chapter 239, 'Communications with Consumers via Artificial Intelligence', to Title 10 of the Maine Revised Statutes. Enacted as amended by committee amendment C-A (H-544) of the Committee on Health Coverage, Insurance and Financial Services. As a non-emergency law of the First Special Session of the 132nd Legislature, which adjourned sine die on 25 June 2025, it took effect on the session's general effective date, 24 September 2025. Maine added a 2026 law restricting AI therapy services (US-ME-02).
