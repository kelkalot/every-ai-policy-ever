Summary

Guideline of the Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology for teachers, school staff and boards of education on the appropriate use of generative AI in primary and secondary schools; it does not uniformly ban or mandate its use. It sets a human-centred approach in which people make the final judgement and take responsibility, and five points for school use: proper use considering safety, including providers' terms of service, age limits and parental consent; information security; protection of personal information, privacy and copyright; fairness and attention to bias; and transparency and accountability to stakeholders.

Status notes

Version 2.0 of 26 Dec 2024 revised the provisional guideline that MEXT published in July 2023 (初等中等教育段階における生成AIの利用に関する暫定的なガイドライン, Ver.1.0), after discussion in a study group on generative AI in primary and secondary education set up in July 2024. MEXT's notices to boards of education on the provisional guideline and on its revision are dated 4 Jul 2023 and 26 Dec 2024. Reference materials include checklists by scenario and examples from generative AI pilot schools. For safety measures by AI developers and providers, the guideline refers to the AI Guidelines for Business and J-AISI's evaluation guide. MEXT's provisional translation is titled 'Guideline for the Use of Generative AI in Elementary and Secondary Education', while MEXT's pages list it as 'Guideline for the Use of Generative AI in Primary and Secondary Education (Ver.2.0)(tentative translation)'.
