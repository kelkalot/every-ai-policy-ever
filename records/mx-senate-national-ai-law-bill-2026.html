Summary

Senate initiative for a national AI law of public order covering public- and private-sector use of AI in Mexico. It would create a regulatory agency for the development, use and application of AI empowered to classify systems by risk level, issue technical guidelines and handle complaints; impose risk assessments, data-management and security obligations on developers and providers; prohibit deceptive data collection, profiling for political persecution and subliminal manipulation; address deepfakes and algorithmic discrimination; and provide for fines, suspension and market withdrawal.
