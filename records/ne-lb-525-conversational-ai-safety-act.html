Summary

Adopts the Conversational Artificial Intelligence Safety Act. Operators of conversational AI services must disclose that the user is interacting with AI whenever a user reasonably could believe they are interacting with a human being, and must apply additional safeguards for minor account holders. Operators must keep a protocol for responding to prompts involving suicidal ideation or self-harm that includes referral to crisis services, and a service may not claim to be designed to provide professional mental or behavioral health care. The Attorney General enforces the provisions through civil actions.

Status notes

LB 525, introduced in the 2025 session by Sen. Mike Jacobson on behalf of Gov. Jim Pillen, mainly protects agricultural data: controllers of such data need written consent agreements with agricultural producers, and from 1 Jan 2027 every new contract on agricultural data must prohibit its sale without the producer's express written consent. The provisions of LB 1185 (Sen. Eliot Bostar), which adopt the Conversational Artificial Intelligence Safety Act, were added to it, and the combined bill passed 49-0.
