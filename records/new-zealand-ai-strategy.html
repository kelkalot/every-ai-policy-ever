Summary

New Zealand's first AI strategy, deliberately focused on practical adoption of AI by businesses rather than high-end development. It takes a light-touch, principles-based approach: existing frameworks such as privacy, consumer protection and human rights law apply and are to be updated as new risks arise, with no regulatory overlay beyond existing law. It confirms New Zealand's adoption of the OECD AI Principles, including robustness, security and safety and accountability, points businesses to the companion Responsible AI Guidance for Businesses, and commits to work in global AI governance fora, including on protecting people from harmful or negligent AI use.

Status notes

Published in July 2025 by the Ministry of Business, Innovation and Employment, with a foreword by Shane Reti, Minister of Science, Innovation and Technology, together with the companion Responsible AI Guidance for Businesses (NZ-03). It complements the work on AI in the public sector led by the Minister for Digitising Government. MBIE states that it used AI to develop aspects of the strategy.
