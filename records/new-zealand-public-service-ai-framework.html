Summary

Non-binding framework for the responsible use of AI across the New Zealand Public Service. It sets a vision, 5 principles informed by the OECD AI Principles (inclusive and sustainable development, human-centred values, transparency and explainability, safety and security, and accountability), the legal and policy context, and 6 pillars of the Government Chief Digital Officer's AI work programme: governance, guardrails, capability, innovation, social licence and global voice. It asks agencies to disclose when they use AI systems and to provide human oversight throughout the AI lifecycle.

Status notes

The framework sits within New Zealand's National AI Strategy, launched in July 2025. It follows Cabinet's decision of June 2024 to promote the OECD AI principles, and it records Cabinet's light-touch, proportionate and risk-based approach to AI regulation, under which further intervention should use existing mechanisms in preference to a standalone AI Act. The page shows 'Last updated 29 January 2025' although it refers to the strategy launched in July 2025.
