Summary

Voluntary guidance that helps businesses, including sole traders, non-profits and individual professionals, use and develop AI systems in a trustworthy way, in line with the OECD AI principles and New Zealand's proportionate, risk-based approach to AI agreed by Cabinet. It covers governance and accountability, legal compliance, risk management, procurement, cybersecurity, privacy, transparency (including labelling AI-generated content), data, model efficacy and human-in-the-loop decision-making, and names risks to cybersecurity, privacy, human rights, workplace culture, the environment, intellectual property and physical safety.

Status notes

Published in July 2025 as the companion to New Zealand's AI strategy (NZ-02). Developed by MBIE with the Government Chief Digital Office and other government agencies, in consultation with members of the AI Forum's Executive Council and Governance Working Group. It is part of a wider suite of responsible AI guidance that includes the Responsible AI Guidance for the Public Service: GenAI, published by the Government Chief Digital Office in the Department of Internal Affairs. A Quickstart Guide for Business Users is published separately.
