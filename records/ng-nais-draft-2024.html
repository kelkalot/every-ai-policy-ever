Summary

Draft national AI strategy (marked 'DRAFT') with a risk-assessment approach (accuracy, bias, transparency, governance), a responsible-AI pillar with a proposed AI Ethics Expert Group or National AI Ethics Commission, and a governance pillar calling for an AI regulatory body and a risk-management framework.

Safety topics

accountability & liabilityevaluation & testinghuman oversight

Scope

Borderline scope Mainly a strategy for AI infrastructure, ecosystem, adoption and skills, with responsible and ethical AI and AI governance as two of its five pillars; included as the draft of Nigeria's national AI strategy.

Status notes

Released Aug 2024 for consultation; superseded by the final NAIS of Sept 2025.
