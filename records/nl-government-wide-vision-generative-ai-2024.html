Summary

Dutch government-wide vision on generative AI, sent to the House of Representatives in January 2024. It maps opportunities and risks, including mis- and disinformation and deepfakes, bias and discrimination, dependence on a few US technology companies, misuse and systemic security risks, and reviews the law, notably the EU AI Act. Four principles: generative AI is developed and applied safely and equitably, serves human welfare and safeguards autonomy, and contributes to sustainability and prosperity. Six action lines: collaborate, monitor developments, shape and apply laws and regulations, increase knowledge and skills, innovate, and strong and clear supervision and enforcement.

Status notes

The report is listed on rijksoverheid.nl with the date 01-01-2024 and was sent to the House of Representatives as an annex to a cover letter listed there on 18 January 2024, from State Secretary Van Huffelen (Kingdom Relations and Digitalisation), Minister Adriaansens (Economic Affairs and Climate Policy) and Minister Dijkgraaf (Education, Culture and Science); the government's English translation of the cover letter is dated 17 January 2024. Other annexes to that letter are an overview of related motions and commitments and a request for advice to the Social and Economic Council (SER) on AI, the future of work and its socio-economic implications. The vision responds to a motion adopted by the House in April 2023. On 22 April 2025 State Secretary Szabó presented to the House a government-wide position on the use of generative AI in government, with a government-wide guide on its responsible use.
