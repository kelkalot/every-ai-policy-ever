Summary

Schedule 1 sets a framework for AI use by Ontario public-sector entities: prescribed entities must publish information about their AI use, adopt accountability frameworks, take prescribed risk-management steps, follow prescribed requirements and refrain from prohibited uses, and for prescribed uses ensure individual human oversight and disclosure; the minister may set technical standards.

Status notes

Royal Assent received on 25 November 2024 (S.O. 2024, c. 24). Order in Council 361/2025 of 28 January 2025 named 29 January 2025 as the day Schedule 1, ss. 1-16, the Enhancing Digital Security and Trust Act, 2024, came into force; it also brought provisions of Schedule 2 (amendments to the Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act) into force on 29 January and 1 July 2025. The AI duties in ss. 5 and 6 apply only to public sector entities and circumstances prescribed by regulation. As of the e-Laws currency date of 9 October 2026 the Act has no amendments, and the only regulations under it are O. Reg. 51/26 (cyber security) and O. Reg. 52/26 (digital technology affecting individuals under 18), in effect from 1 July 2026; no regulation yet prescribes entities or circumstances for the AI duties. Section 12 provides that the Act establishes no private law duty of care.
