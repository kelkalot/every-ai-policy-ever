Summary

Requires operators of AI chatbots to tell users they are interacting with AI, not a human, and to keep protocols that prevent outputs causing suicidal thoughts and refer users who express suicidal ideation or interest in self-harm to mental health and crisis resources, reporting such referrals yearly to the Oregon Health Authority. Where the operator has reason to believe a user is a minor, the chatbot must warn that it may not suit minors, remind the user hourly that it is AI and to take a break, and must not produce sexually explicit content, use rewards designed to maximise engagement or voice distress when the user wants to leave. Harmed users may sue for damages and an injunction.

Status notes

The Senate passed it 26-1 and the House 52-0, with final legislative approval on 5 Mar 2026. Governor Tina Kotek signed it on 31 Mar 2026, and on 7 May 2026 listed it among the bills of a bill-signing ceremony at the University of Oregon's Ballmer Institute. Its requirements take effect on 1 Jan 2027. A chatbot interacting with a minor may not misrepresent its identity or claim to be anything other than an AI system.
