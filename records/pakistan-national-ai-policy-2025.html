Summary

Pakistan's national AI policy, approved in July 2025, with six pillars from innovation and skills to a secure AI ecosystem, infrastructure and international partnerships. Its governance measures include an AI Council chaired by the IT Minister, an AI Directorate under the National Commission for Personal Data Protection to issue regulatory guidelines on generative AI, disinformation and privacy breaches, human oversight of critical AI operations in high-risk scenarios, mandatory disclosure of AI use with a public register of public-sector AI systems, lifecycle evaluations and impact assessments of high-impact systems, third-party audits with redress, and regulatory sandboxes.

Status notes

MoITT's policies page lists the policy as approved on 31 July 2025 and still lists the earlier draft National Artificial Intelligence Policy of 25 June 2022 as a draft. The policy cites the UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI and the UN Sustainable Development Goals, and is meant to work together with the draft Personal Data Protection Act, the Pakistan Cloud First Policy, the Digital Pakistan Policy and the Digital Nation Pakistan Act. Its action matrix sets most targets for 2026, including the AI Directorate under the National Commission for Personal Data Protection and an integrated regulatory sandbox for AI led by the SECP; the policy text also mentions independent oversight bodies, including an AI ethics board.
