Bill promoting the use of artificial intelligence for the economic and social development of the country (Chamber of Deputies, 2025)

Que promueve el uso de la inteligencia artificial en favor del desarrollo económico y social del país

Summary

Chamber of Deputies bill declaring the development and implementation of AI of national interest as a strategic tool for modernising the country's sectors. In 20 articles it would make the ICT ministry (MITIC) the enforcement authority, in charge of national AI policies, supervision and a National Register of AI Systems; set principles for ethical AI use stressing privacy, prevention of bias and discrimination, and algorithmic transparency; prohibit AI for indiscriminate surveillance; require ethical audits of systems affecting fundamental rights; set sanctions for misuse; and create a National Committee on AI Ethics and Regulation, with tax incentives, research and education measures.

Status notes

Presented in the Chamber of Deputies shortly before 1 May 2025 by five deputies; a press report of 1 May 2025 also names Deputies Carlos Núñez Salinas and Johanna Ortega among its sponsors. The Chamber's bulletin item of 2 May 2025 gives the title as 'Que promueve el uso de la Inteligencia Artificial (IA) en favor del desarrollo económico, social, científico y cultural del país'; later references give the shorter title. The Committee on Social Equity and Equal Rights of Men and Women, chaired by Deputy Abed, convened a public hearing on the bill for 4 Jun 2025. On 27 Oct 2025 the Committee on National Defence, Security, Intelligence and Internal Order continued studying it; it still awaited technical opinions from MITIC, which would be the main regulator, and from the Ministry of the Interior. A separate Senate bill regulating AI systems, presented in May 2025, is PY-01.
