Summary

Statement of the AI Action Summit in Paris that sets six priorities: accessible AI to reduce digital divides; AI that is open, inclusive, transparent, ethical, safe, secure and trustworthy; innovation without market concentration; AI that shapes work and labour markets positively; sustainable AI; and international cooperation on governance. It commends the Bletchley and Seoul summits' work on AI safety and notes the voluntary commitments made there, says the signatories will keep addressing AI risks to information integrity and continue work on AI transparency, and reaffirms the commitment to a UN Global Dialogue on AI governance and an Independent International Scientific Panel on AI.

Status notes

Published on 11 February 2025, the second day of the AI Action Summit, held in Paris on 10 and 11 February 2025. The signatory list names 63 countries, the African Union Commission and the EU; the United States and the United Kingdom are not on it. A footnote limits the statement to civil applications and use of AI. Founding members launched a Public Interest AI Platform and Incubator under it. The International AI Safety Report 2025 (INT-09) was published ahead of the summit.
