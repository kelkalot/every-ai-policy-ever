Summary

Regulation of Law 31814 with a risk-based regime. Prohibited ('improper') uses include manipulative influence on decisions, autonomous lethal capability without human supervision in the civilian sphere, mass surveillance without legal basis, inferring sensitive traits from biometrics, real-time biometric identification to categorise people in public spaces (except identity authentication and investigation of listed serious crimes) and crime prediction by profiling. High-risk uses (critical national assets, evaluating minors, employment, social programmes, credit, health) require transparency, human supervision and impact assessment (mandatory for public entities, voluntary for private).

Status notes

Issued on 8 September 2025 and published in El Peruano on 9 September 2025. The decree enters into force 90 business days after publication, which falls in January 2026, except the First, Second, Fourth and Fifth Final Complementary Provisions of the Regulation, which applied from the day after publication. The transparency duties (Article 25) and the Title VI obligations are phased in: public sector within 1 year (executive, legislature, judiciary, autonomous constitutional bodies), 2 years (EsSalud, regional governments, public universities, public enterprises) and 3 years (local governments of types A to C; optional for types D to G); private sector within 1 year (health, education, justice, security, economy and finance), 2 years (transport, commerce, labour), 3 years (production, agriculture, energy, mining) and 4 years (all other uses); small enterprises 2 years and micro-enterprises 3 years. Public administration entities must also run security audits before and during operation, manage AI incidents and use NTP-ISO/IEC 42001:2025 when developing AI systems. AI used for national defence and security is outside the Regulation's scope provided it respects the principles of Article 7 (Article 4(b)); personal use is also excluded. Systems that are neither improper nor high-risk are of 'acceptable risk' (Article 22.2).
