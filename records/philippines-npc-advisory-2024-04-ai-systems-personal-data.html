Summary

Advisory of the Philippine National Privacy Commission on how the Data Privacy Act of 2012, its implementing rules and Commission issuances apply when personal data are processed to develop or deploy AI systems, including training and testing. Controllers must explain the purpose, inputs, risks, expected output and impact of the processing; remain accountable, including for processors; set up governance such as privacy impact assessments, continuous monitoring and an AI ethics board; provide meaningful human intervention and a way to contest automated decisions that pose a significant risk; identify and limit bias; avoid 'AI washing'; and keep data-subject rights exercisable throughout.

Status notes

Signed by Privacy Commissioner John Henry D. Naga and Deputy Privacy Commissioners Leandro Angelo Y. Aguirre and Nerissa N. de Jesus. Issued under Section 7(g) of the Data Privacy Act, which empowers the Commission to publish a guide to all laws relating to data protection, and Section 9 of the Act's implementing rules. Its description of AI systems cites the EU High-Level Expert Group's Ethics Guidelines for Trustworthy AI, the OECD Recommendation on Artificial Intelligence, ISO/IEC 22989:2022 and NIST AI 600-1. The advisory states that publicly available personal data keep the Act's protection, and that incorporating personal data into data sets does not by itself make data-subject requests unreasonable.
