Summary

Guidance from the Philippine data protection regulator on how the Data Privacy Act, its rules and NPC issuances apply to AI systems that process personal data during training, testing and deployment. It restates the general privacy principles for AI, the lawful criteria for processing, transparency duties (including information about automated decision-making and profiling), the obligation to run privacy impact assessments and implement security measures proportionate to AI risks, and data-subject rights, holding that controllers cannot treat requests as unreasonable if they provide no mechanism to exercise them. Doubts are to be resolved in favour of data subjects.

Status notes

Signed by Privacy Commissioner John Henry D. Naga and Deputy Commissioners Leandro Angelo Y. Aguirre and Nerissa N. de Jesus. NPC advisories interpret binding law but are not themselves regulations. Issued alongside NPC Advisory No. 2024-03 on child-oriented transparency. The Philippines has no AI-specific statute; dozens of AI bills are pending in the 20th Congress.
