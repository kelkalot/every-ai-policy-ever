Alerts and Other Measures Concerning the Use of Generative AI Services

Summary

Alert of the Personal Information Protection Commission on generative AI services, weighing individuals' rights against the public benefits of new technology. Businesses and administrative bodies entering personal information in prompts must confirm this stays within the specified purpose of use. If the data are then handled for purposes other than producing the response, a business acting without prior consent, or an administrative body, may breach the Act on the Protection of Personal Information, so both should check that the provider does not use them for machine learning. Users are warned that inputs may be used for training and outputs may contain inaccurate personal information.

Status notes

Annex 2 summarises the alert the PPC issued on 1 June 2023 under Article 147 of the Act on the Protection of Personal Information to OpenAI, L.L.C. and OpenAI OpCo, LLC, which develop and provide ChatGPT: not to acquire sensitive personal information (要配慮個人情報) without consent, with four measures for information collected for machine learning; not to handle sensitive information entered by users who opted out of training; and to notify or publish the purposes of use in Japanese. The PPC said it would keep watching generative AI services and might issue further alerts.
