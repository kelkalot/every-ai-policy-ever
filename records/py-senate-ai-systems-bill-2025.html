Que regula y promueve la creación, desarrollo, innovación e implementación de Sistemas de Inteligencia Artificial (IA)

Summary

Senate bill to regulate and promote AI systems through risk categorisation. Before being marketed or put into service, every AI system would undergo a preliminary evaluation by its provider to classify its risk; applications with excessive risk would be prohibited and high-risk applications subject to stricter controls. The bill sets transparency and bias-mitigation measures, additional measures for high-risk and government AI systems, and an algorithmic impact assessment procedure. Persons affected by AI systems would have rights to prior information, explanation, challenge of decisions with significant effects, human participation, non-discrimination and data protection.

Status notes

Presented by Sen. Basilio Núñez, President of Congress, with six other senators, and referred to the Senate's advisory committees on Constitutional Affairs; National Defence and Public Force; Legislation, Codification, Justice and Labour; Human Rights; Industry and Commerce; and Science, Technology, Innovation and Future. A co-sponsor, Sen. Natalicio Chase, dates the presentation to 5 May 2025 and describes the bill as based, like the EU's regulation, on risks and fundamental rights. Its explanatory statement reviews AI legislation in the European Union, China and the United States. A separate Chamber of Deputies bill on promoting AI for economic and social development is PY-02.
