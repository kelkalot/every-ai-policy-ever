Summary

Voluntary, non-binding guidance from Qatar's Ministry of Communications and Information Technology for developers and deployers of AI systems that affect the public. It sets eight principles: do no harm; ensure robustness, security and safety; avoid perpetuating bias and discrimination; protect the environment; safeguard privacy; promote transparency; develop a human-centred approach; and assign ultimate accountability to humans. It calls for risk evaluations before deployment, robustness testing, continuous monitoring, discrimination impact assessments, bias audits, human intervention and appeal for consequential decisions, and external audits of systems that inform significant decisions.

Status notes

Posted on the government e-participation portal Sharek on 16 May 2024 together with the companion 'Artificial Intelligence in Qatar—Principles and Guidelines for Ethical Use', which addresses users of AI systems such as private companies, government agencies, the public and students. Both documents are to be updated every one to two years. The guidelines are aligned with UNESCO and OECD standards and refer to the National Cyber Security Agency's 'Guidelines for Secure Adoption and Usage of Artificial Intelligence' for risk evaluation and security standards.
