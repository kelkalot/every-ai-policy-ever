Summary

Outcome document of the second summit on Responsible AI in the Military Domain (REAIM), held in Seoul on 9-10 September 2024 and co-organised by the Republic of Korea's foreign and defence ministries. Endorsed at the closing session and supported by 61 states, the declarative Blueprint lays out a roadmap for establishing norms for AI in the military domain. It suggests principles and a framework for future governance, emphasising that being responsible entails complying with international law, holding humans responsible and accountable, ensuring reliable and trustworthy AI, maintaining appropriate human involvement and improving explainability.

Status notes

The Blueprint was endorsed at the closing session that followed the ministerial roundtable of 10 September 2024, which brought together government representatives from over 90 states. The summit's plenary sessions covered the implications of AI for international peace and security, including conflict dynamics and the proliferation of weapons of mass destruction, the responsible application of AI in the military domain, and its future governance. The 2026 REAIM Pathways to Action (INT-18) builds on the Blueprint, 'advanced at the REAIM Summit 2024 in Seoul', and on the 2023 Call to Action (INT-14).
