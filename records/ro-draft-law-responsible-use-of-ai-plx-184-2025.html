Summary

Private members' bill by SOS România senators and deputies that would regulate AI use in Romania. AI in public administration would have to be transparent and periodically audited, with no administrative decisions taken without human intervention; AI could not pronounce court judgments, law-enforcement AI would need certification and AI-based medical decisions validation by a specialist. People would gain rights to an explanation and to human interaction. It bans harmful deepfakes and political profiling without consent, makes platforms label AI content and sets prison terms of two to five years and fines of up to RON 100 million. The Senate rejected it in June 2025.

Status notes

Registered at the Senate on 31 March 2025 (B114) and presented to its Standing Bureau as L140/2025 on 14 May 2025, with the Senate as first chamber. The Senate rejected it on 2 June 2025 (84 for, 40 against, 2 abstentions) after negative opinions from the Legislative Council, the Economic and Social Council, the National Audiovisual Council, ANCOM and every consulted committee, and sent it to the Chamber of Deputies, the decisional chamber, where it was registered as PL-x 184/2025 on 4 June 2025 and sent for report to the Legal Committee and the Committee for Information Technology and Communications. The latter voted by majority to reject it on 16 September 2025, its chairman pointing to Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 and to the National AI Strategy 2024-2027 approved by Government Decision 832/2024; the Chamber received the two committees' rejection report on 26 November 2025, and on 17 October 2026 the bill was waiting to be put on the plenary agenda. It would be an organic law and enter into force 90 days after publication in the Monitorul Oficial. It defines AI as any computer system able to perform cognitive functions such as learning, reasoning and decision-making, and makes AI developers responsible for the impact of their algorithms, with annual independent audits of AI used in decision-making.
