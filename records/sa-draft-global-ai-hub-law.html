Summary

Draft law that the Communications, Space and Technology Commission (CST) put to public consultation from 14 April to 14 May 2025 to attract investment in data centres and AI. It aims to make the Kingdom a global digital hub and to enable sovereign data centres that ensure service continuity and data sovereignty beyond borders. It sets three models: a 'Private Center' for countries around the world to host their data and services in the Kingdom, and an 'Extended Center' and a 'Virtual Center' for major AI and cloud computing companies to host their worldwide customers in the Kingdom at levels of sovereignty and privileges suited to those customers.
