Summary

Two companion SDAIA guidelines on generative AI. The government version governs the use of government data in GenAI tools: entities must apply SDAIA's AI Ethics Principles, must not use GenAI for critical decisions affecting individuals or the Kingdom's vital interests, must human-review outputs, must not enter restricted or classified data, and must rate tools on a four-level risk scale (little or no, limited, high with conformity assessment, unacceptable); data offices approve tools and audit training data, and AI-generated content is to be disclosed. The public version sets seven principles, including reliability and safety with human-oversight triggers, and lists risks and mitigations.

Status notes

SDAIA lists the two documents among its laws and regulations as two versions of one 'Generative AI Guideline', the first for government employees and the second for the public. Both PDFs carry 'May 2025' and 'Version Number 1' in their document-control tables, with document type 'Regulatory Documents'. The government version describes its rules on government data as mandatory and says it may be updated as needed; the public version serves as guidance for developers and users of generative AI in the Kingdom. Both apply SDAIA's AI Ethics Principles (SA-01); SDAIA's deepfakes guidelines (SA-03) refer to the public version.
