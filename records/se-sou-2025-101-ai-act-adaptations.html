Summary

Report of the government inquiry on the EU AI Regulation proposing a new Swedish act and ordinance to supplement it. It proposes eleven market surveillance authorities and two notifying authorities, Swedac and the Medical Products Agency. The Post and Telecom Authority (PTS) would hold main responsibility, coordinate and be the single point of contact, and the Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) would lead on prohibited practices. It also proposes reprimands and administrative fines that can be imposed on public authorities too, confidentiality rules including protection of incident reporting, an AI regulatory sandbox set up by PTS, and approval of testing in real-world conditions.

Status notes

Report of the Inquiry on the AI Regulation, a special inquiry the Government appointed on 19 September 2024 under committee terms of reference Dir. 2024:83. It was published on 6 October 2025 and sent out for consultation on 11 November 2025 (Fi2025/01969). It proposes that the supplementary act, the supplementary ordinance and the other amendments enter into force on 2 August 2026, and it recommends that the Government designate market surveillance and notifying authorities at least temporarily while its proposals are prepared. The report includes a summary in English. As of October 2026 the Government's page for the report lists no referral to the Council on Legislation and no government bill.
