Seoul Declaration for safe, innovative and inclusive AI by participants attending the Leaders' Session: AI Seoul Summit, 21 May 2024

Record INT-04

Summary

Leaders of ten countries and the EU treat AI safety, innovation and inclusivity as inter-related goals and call for interoperable AI governance frameworks, in line with a risk-based approach, for the safe, secure and trustworthy design, development, deployment and use of AI. They support creating or expanding AI safety institutes, research programmes and supervisory bodies and networks between them, welcome the annexed Seoul Statement of Intent on AI safety science, recognise the particular responsibility of organisations developing frontier AI, note the Frontier AI Safety Commitments and back the operationalisation of the Hiroshima Process Code of Conduct.

Status notes

Agreed at the Leaders' Session of the AI Seoul Summit on 21 May 2024. The summit, co-hosted by the UK and the Republic of Korea on 21 and 22 May 2024, built on the AI Safety Summit at Bletchley Park (INT-03). The Seoul Statement of Intent toward International Cooperation on AI Safety Science (INT-05) is annexed to it. It looks forward to the third gathering, the AI Action Summit in France (INT-01).
