Seoul Ministerial Statement for advancing AI safety, innovation and inclusivity: AI Seoul Summit 2024

Summary

Ministers of 27 countries and the EU recognise their role in establishing frameworks to manage risks from frontier AI models, in promoting credible external evaluations, and in identifying, with the private sector, civil society and academia, thresholds at which unmitigated risks would be severe. Their examples are capabilities that meaningfully assist non-state actors with chemical or biological weapons, and capabilities or propensities to evade human oversight through manipulation, deception or autonomous replication. Through their AI safety institutes they plan to share best practices and evaluation datasets and to collaborate on safety testing guidelines.

Status notes

Agreed at the Ministers' Session of the AI Seoul Summit on 22 May 2024; the Republic of Korea and the United Kingdom were the summit's co-chairs. Signatories include the US, UK, India, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Türkiye, Kenya, Nigeria, Rwanda and the EU; China is not on the list. Ministers planned to develop proposals on thresholds and capabilities for consideration before the AI Action Summit in France (INT-01), and looked forward to the final International Scientific Report on the Safety of Advanced AI, whose interim report (INT-08) they noted.
