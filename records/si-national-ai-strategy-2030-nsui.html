Summary

Slovenia's national AI strategy, adopted by the Government on 5 March 2026 as successor to the 2021 programme NpUI 2025. Its guiding principles include responsibility, security and trust; its five goals include sovereign and trustworthy AI and security, resilience and defence through AI. The 'ethics and regulation' building block commits to a framework for the AI Act under the ZIUDHPUI (competent bodies and sandboxes by 2026), a National Council for Ethics in AI in 2026, fundamental-rights impact assessments of high-risk systems with guidelines by 2027, sectoral ethical codes and technical guidelines with risk-management protocols, transparency and security testing, and compliance tools.

Status notes

The Government's news release of 5 March 2026 says the Government adopted the strategy at its session that day. The strategy succeeds the national programme NpUI 2025, adopted in May 2021. Its first action plan, setting measures, responsible bodies, timelines, funding and indicators, is to be prepared within nine months of adoption under the Ministry of Digital Transformation. The strategy sets ten guiding principles, five strategic objectives and six horizontal building blocks, and names the Ministry of Digital Transformation as its national manager, with an interministerial coordination group, a Government Council for AI and an AI observatory. Its draft was in public consultation for 30 days from 17 November 2025, and the final draft was scheduled for the Economic and Social Council on 16 January 2026 and for adoption by the Government on 22 January 2026. For fundamental-rights impact assessment it refers to the Council of Europe's methodology for human rights, democracy and the rule of law.
