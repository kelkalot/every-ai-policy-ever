Summary

Designates the Digital Trust Centre, a national centre hosted at Nanyang Technological University, as Singapore's AI Safety Institute. The AISI drives Singapore's AI evaluation and testing work to help address gaps in global AI safety science. IMDA is the overall government agency for it, responsible for baseline policy and international engagement, while the Centre does the technical work. Its work covers technical testing, including multilingual and agentic AI testing and joint testing with international partners, studies of AI's societal impact such as emotional dependency, and international partnerships, including capacity building in ASEAN.

Status notes

IMDA's factsheet announcing the designation carries the dateline Singapore, 22 May 2024; IMDA last updated the page on 10 Oct 2024. IMDA partners the Digital Trust Centre to drive the AISI's technical work. The Centre is funded by a $50 million grant from the National Research Foundation and IMDA, starting from 1 Oct 2022. The AISI's joint testing is done with other members of the international Network for Advanced AI Measurement, Evaluation and Science (NAAIMES).
