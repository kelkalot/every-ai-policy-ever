AI Verify – AI Governance Testing Framework and Toolkit

Summary

Government AI governance testing framework and software toolkit, launched as a minimum viable product for an international industry pilot. Companies self-test their AI systems with standardised technical tests and process checks and generate reports for stakeholders. The pilot covers 8 of 11 internationally accepted AI ethics principles, grouped in five pillars: transparency on the use of AI, understanding how a model reaches decisions, safety and resilience, fairness, and management and oversight. It sets no ethical standards and does not guarantee that a tested system is free from risks or biases.

Status notes

Announced by Minister for Communications and Information Josephine Teo at the World Economic Forum Annual Meeting in Davos. MDDI's newsroom dates the joint press release of the Ministry of Communications and Information, IMDA and the PDPC 26 May 2022; IMDA's copy carries the dateline Davos, 25 May 2022. Developed under the guidance of the Advisory Council on the Ethical Use of AI and Data, it was tested or reviewed by 10 companies before launch. The pilot toolkit worked with a subset of common supervised models (binary classification and regression), mainly tabular datasets and models of about 2GB or less; it did not support unsupervised models.
