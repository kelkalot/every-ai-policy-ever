Summary

Outcome document of the 2025 Singapore Conference on AI: International Scientific Exchange on AI Safety, drafted by an expert committee and revised with feedback from more than 100 participants from 11 countries, from academia, AI developers, governments and civil society. Focused on general-purpose AI and building on the International AI Safety Report, it uses a defence-in-depth model to group technical AI safety research priorities into three areas: risk assessment, including metrology and enablers for third-party audits; development of trustworthy, reliable and secure systems (specification, design, verification); and control, covering monitoring, intervention and societal resilience.

Status notes

The exchange was held on 26 Apr 2025. An Expert Planning Committee (Dawn Song, Lan Xue, Luke Ong, Max Tegmark, Stuart Russell, Tegan Maharaj, Ya-Qin Zhang and Yoshua Bengio) drafted the Consensus for consultation, and it was revised after several rounds of participant feedback. It is dated 8 May 2025 and is a living document that continues to welcome views from the global research community. A second edition, The 2026 Singapore Consensus on Global AI Safety Research Priorities (July 2026), came out of the 2nd International Scientific Exchange on AI Safety in May 2026: it builds on the 2025 report, makes societal resilience a fourth pillar and adds a companion report on agentic risk management.
