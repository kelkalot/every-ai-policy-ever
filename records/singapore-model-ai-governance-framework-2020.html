Summary

Voluntary framework of Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission for organisations deploying AI at scale, in any sector and regardless of technology or algorithm. It turns common AI ethics principles into practices in four areas: internal governance structures and measures; the level of human involvement in AI-augmented decisions, set with a matrix of the probability and severity of harm (human-in-the-loop, over-the-loop or out-of-the-loop); operations management, covering data, explainability, repeatability, robustness, traceability, reproducibility and auditability; and stakeholder interaction and communication.

Status notes

The first edition was released on 23 Jan 2019 and the second on 21 Jan 2020, both at the World Economic Forum Annual Meeting in Davos. The second edition added industry examples, clarified the human-over-the-loop approach, added robustness, reproducibility and auditability measures, replaced the use-case annex with a separate Compendium of Use Cases and limited algorithm audits to a regulator's request. IMDA and the PDPC developed a companion Implementation and Self-Assessment Guide for Organisations (ISAGO) with the World Economic Forum. Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2012, the IMDA is designated as the PDPC.
