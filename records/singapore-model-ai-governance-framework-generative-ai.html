Summary

Framework for generative AI developed by IMDA and the AI Verify Foundation, expanding the Model AI Governance Framework for traditional AI. It sets out nine dimensions to be looked at in totality: accountability along the AI development chain, data quality and contentious training data, trusted development and deployment with 'food label'-type transparency, incident reporting, third-party testing and assurance, security against new threat vectors, content provenance such as watermarking, safety and alignment R&D, and AI for the public good. It calls on policymakers, industry, the research community and the public to do their part.

Status notes

IMDA and the AI Verify Foundation released a proposed framework for international views on 16 Jan 2024, announced at Davos. They received over 70 responses from local and international companies, technology multinationals, audit firms and government agencies, and finalised the framework on 30 May 2024, when IMDA also announced that Singapore would lead, with Rwanda, the development of an AI Governance Playbook for the Digital Forum of Small States. The framework builds on the June 2023 Discussion Paper on Generative AI by IMDA, Aicadium and the AI Verify Foundation, and is meant as a first step: implementation guidelines and resources are to follow under each of the nine dimensions.
