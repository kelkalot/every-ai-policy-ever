National Strategy and Roadmap of Senegal on Artificial Intelligence to 2028

Summary

Strategy to 2028 with four objectives, six orientations and 52 actions under a vision of ethical, trustworthy AI as a catalyst of the Plan Sénégal Émergent. Orientation 6, 'AI in full confidence', sets out sixteen governance actions: a national consultation, an audit of regional and international ethical and legal recommendations, categorising AI types by their risks to human rights and societal well-being, a balanced and flexible AI regulation, a prefiguration body and then a regulator for emerging technologies, a national ethics committee, regulatory sandboxes and mandatory impact assessments for designated AI types, with soft law in the interim.

Scope

Borderline scope Mainly a strategy for AI skills, research, an AI cluster and adoption; included as Senegal's national AI strategy, one of whose six orientations sets out AI governance: risk categorisation, regulation, a regulator and an ethics committee.

Status notes

The published document is a summary version ('version résumée', 20 pages) dated 2023, produced with technical assistance from Expertise France under the AU-EU D4D Hub project. The minister presented the strategy publicly on 14 September 2023 as a first restitution of the work, open to public contributions, and announced that a final version would be submitted to the head of state for political validation. The OECD.AI entry gives 2024 as its start year and lists it as active. Besides orientation 6, the strategy proposes reaffirming in law the principles that multinational platforms must respect, drawing on the EU's Digital Services Act, Digital Markets Act and AI Act, and plans to train about 90,000 people in data science and AI by 2028.
