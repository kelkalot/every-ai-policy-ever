Summary

TC260 practice guide on how individual users can use AI services over the internet securely. Users should recognise AI's limits such as errors and hallucinations, avoid over-reliance and replacing real social contact, and watch for misuse such as cloned voices of relatives or forged official notices. They should use services from regular channels and generative AI built on filed large models, check agreements and permissions, protect sensitive information, cross-check outputs, treat legal, medical or financial outputs as reference only, and not induce harmful outputs, impersonate others or remove AI content labels.

Version v1.0-202608, released with notice 网安秘字〔2026〕105号 of 24 Aug 2026. It also serves as a reference for technology providers, developers and operators of AI services. For deploying agents it refers users to TC260-PG-20266A, and for the style of content labels to chapter 5 of GB 45438-2025 (CN-08).
