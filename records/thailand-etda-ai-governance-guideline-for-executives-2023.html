Summary

Voluntary governance framework from Thailand's AI Governance Center for organisations adopting AI. It links the national AI strategy, the six Thai AI ethics principles (transparency and accountability, law and ethics, competitiveness and sustainability, reliability, fairness, security and privacy) and practical toolkits. Three components: an AI governance structure (governance council, roles, competency building), AI strategy (responsible-AI strategy and risk management referencing NIST and ISO standards and human-in/over/out-of-the-loop choices) and AI operation (lifecycle controls from design to retirement, user information and feedback). Toolkits cover readiness and risk assessment.

Status notes

Listed under 2023 among the recommendations and reports of ETDA's AI Governance Center. The English document is an 18-slide presentation developed by the AIGC team; its cover spells the title 'Guideline for Executive' (singular), while a slide inside reads 'AI Governance Guideline for Executives'. It names ISO/IEC 23894:2023 and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0) as international frameworks organisations can adapt, and bases the choice of human involvement on the level of risk, the severity of potential harm, whether impacts can be remedied and whether human intervention is feasible. Its toolkits are an AI Readiness Assessment, an AI Use Case Canvas and a Risk Assessment.
