Summary

Eight-article private member's bill setting a regulatory framework for the development and use of AI. It defines AI and AI operators (providers, distributor-users, importers, distributors) and makes safety, transparency, fairness, accountability and privacy mandatory principles. It requires risk assessment, with special measures for high-risk systems, which must be registered with the relevant supervisory authorities and undergo conformity assessment, empowers those authorities to audit compliance, and sets fines of up to TRY 35 million or 7% of annual turnover for prohibited AI practices, TRY 15 million or 3% for breaches of obligations and TRY 7.5 million or 1.5% for false information.

Borderline scope Private member's bill that has stayed in committee since June 2024 with no further action; included as an AI-specific bill before the Grand National Assembly.

Status notes

Received by the TBMM Presidency on 24 June 2024 and referred on 25 June 2024 to the Industry, Trade, Energy, Natural Resources, Information and Technology Committee as lead committee and to the Justice Committee as secondary committee, where it remains. The bill would enter into force on publication. It does not define which systems are high-risk, name the supervisory authorities or list the prohibited practices its fines refer to. 'Distributor-user' renders dağıtıcı/kullanıcı, defined as a person who distributes AI systems commercially or uses them in their own activities. A later private member's bill on AI provisions in the Turkish Penal Code and other laws (2/3358) is TR-02.
