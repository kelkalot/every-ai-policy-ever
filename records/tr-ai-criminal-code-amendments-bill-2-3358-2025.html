Summary

Private member's bill amending the Penal Code and Laws No. 5651 (internet), 6698 (data protection), 5809 (e-communications) and 7545 (cybersecurity). It makes a user who directs an AI system to a criminal act its perpetrator; requires removal or blocking within six hours of AI content that violates personality rights, threatens public security or is a deepfake; mandates a non-removable 'Produced by Artificial Intelligence' label on deepfakes (fines TRY 500,000-5 million); and obliges providers to ensure transparency and auditability of training data, content verification, hallucination controls and human-approved oversight in high-risk uses. ICT regulator BTK gets urgent blocking powers.

Status notes

Signed by the sponsor on 23 July 2025, received by the TBMM on 7 November 2025 and registered on 10 November 2025 as Esas No. 2/3358, with the Industry, Trade, Energy, Natural Resources, Information and Technology Committee as lead committee and the Justice and Digital Media committees as secondary committees. The cover letter describes it as a bill amending Turkish Penal Code No. 5237 and certain laws. It has 11 articles and would enter into force on publication. Article 1 adds a definition of AI systems to Law No. 5651. It also requires datasets used in AI applications to respect anonymity, non-discrimination and legitimacy, treating discriminatory datasets as a data-security breach, raises the penalty for a developer whose system's design or training enabled the offence by half, and adds insult, threat and crimes against humanity to the offences for which internet content is removed or blocked.
