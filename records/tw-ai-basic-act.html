Summary

Framework law setting seven principles for government AI policy: sustainable development and well-being, human autonomy, privacy protection and data governance, cybersecurity and safety, transparency and explainability, fairness and non-discrimination, and accountability. The government must prevent AI uses that infringe life, body, liberty or property or involve discrimination or misleading information, and regulators must restrict or prohibit them. High-risk applications must carry warnings. MODA must promote an AI risk taxonomy and assessment framework and assessment tools, liability and remedies for high-risk AI must be defined, and agencies must risk-assess their own AI use.

Status notes

Enacted in full (20 articles) by presidential order promulgated on 14 Jan 2026 (ROC 115-01-14), and in force from promulgation (Art. 20). Agencies must review the laws and administrative measures they administer and make, amend or repeal them within two years of the Act's effective date (Art. 18). The Executive Yuan must set up a National AI Strategic Committee, convened by the Premier with the NSTC handling its staff work, which formulates the National AI Development Guidelines and meets at least once a year (Art. 6). High-risk applications are those a central sector authority, in consultation with the Ministry of Digital Affairs, identifies as such (Art. 5(2)).
