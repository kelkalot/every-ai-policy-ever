Roadmap for the Regulation of Artificial Intelligence in Ukraine

Summary

Roadmap presented by Ukraine's Ministry of Digital Transformation on 7 October 2023 to help companies prepare for a law modelled on the EU AI Act and to help citizens learn to protect themselves from AI risks. It takes a bottom-up approach: before a law is adopted, businesses receive tools to prepare for future requirements, namely voluntary codes of conduct on the ethical use of AI, a White Paper on the approach, terms and stages of regulation, and general and sector-specific recommendations. It seeks a balance between business interests and the protection of citizens' rights, and was drafted with the ministry's AI expert committee.

Status notes

Followed by the White Paper on AI regulation (UA-02). By 2026 the ministry reported 11 recommendations for safe work with algorithms in key sectors, a regulatory sandbox that received 90 applications in its first year, version 2.0 of its AI glossary (2026), and Ukraine's signature of the Bletchley Declaration and the Council of Europe Framework Convention on AI (COE-01). A joint working group on the legal regulation of AI has held its first meeting and is to draft, during 2026, an AI law that brings Ukraine's rules in line with the EU's.
