Summary

Charter setting twelve principles for the development and use of AI in the UAE, to achieve the goals of the UAE Strategy for Artificial Intelligence: a beneficial human-machine relationship; safety, with AI systems held to the highest safety standards and risky systems modified or removed; addressing algorithmic bias; data privacy; transparency; human oversight to correct errors and biases; governance and accountability; technological excellence; human commitment; peaceful coexistence with AI; AI awareness for an inclusive future; and compliance with international treaties and local laws.

Status notes

The charter states that it aims to achieve the strategic goals of the UAE Strategy for Artificial Intelligence (AE-01). Its objectives include ethical and responsible use of AI, privacy and data security, transparency and accountability, and awareness and education; its expected outcomes include innovation and investment, better health, education and government services, and environmental sustainability.
