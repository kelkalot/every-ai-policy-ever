Summary

Independent report led by Matt Clifford, with 50 recommendations for government to grow the UK's AI sector and drive AI adoption across the economy and public services. Its section on safe and trusted AI (recommendations 23 to 30) calls for continued support for the AI Safety Institute's research on model evaluations, foundational safety and societal resilience, says any regulation of frontier models should preserve the institute's capability, and recommends funding regulators' AI capabilities, regulatory sandboxes and new AI assurance tools.

Scope

Borderline scope Mainly an economic growth and adoption plan with one section on regulation, safety and assurance; included as the UK government's flagship AI plan of January 2025.

Status notes

Led by Matt Clifford CBE, Chair of the Advanced Research and Invention Agency, whom the Secretary of State for Science, Innovation and Technology commissioned to devise the plan; presented to Parliament as Command Paper CP 1241 in January 2025. In the government's response of the same day (CP 1242) the Prime Minister endorsed the plan, and the government agreed to the recommendations, partially agreeing to two and answering the one on text and data mining with its consultation. On recommendation 23 it said DSIT would consult on legislation on the critical risks of the most powerful AI models and that it intended to establish the AI Safety Institute as a statutory body.
