Summary

White paper setting out the UK's context-based approach to AI regulation. Existing regulators are to apply five cross-sectoral principles (safety, security and robustness; appropriate transparency and explainability; fairness; accountability and governance; contestability and redress), issued at first on a non-statutory basis, with a statutory duty on regulators to have due regard to them anticipated later if needed. New central functions, including risk monitoring and horizon scanning, support regulators, and the paper considers how foundation models fit the framework.

Status notes

Presented to Parliament as Command Paper CP 815 and published with an impact assessment and a consultation, which closed on 21 June 2023. A correction slip of 4 July 2023 corrected minor errors in Annex C, and the GOV.UK page was last updated on 3 August 2023. GOV.UK shows it as published under the 2022 to 2024 Sunak Conservative government.
