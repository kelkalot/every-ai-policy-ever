Summary

A Private Members' Bill presented in the House of Commons under the Ten Minute Rule by Alex Sobel MP in September 2026. According to its long title, it would prohibit the development, deployment and operation of artificial superintelligence systems and establish monitoring and control powers in respect of such systems.
