Summary

The first General Assembly resolution on regulating artificial intelligence. It calls on Member States and stakeholders to refrain from or cease using AI systems that cannot be operated in compliance with international human rights law or that pose undue risks to human rights, and affirms that the rights people have offline must also be protected online throughout the life cycle of AI systems. It urges States, the private sector, civil society, research organisations and the media to develop and support regulatory and governance approaches for safe, secure and trustworthy AI, and to help developing countries close the digital divide.

Status notes

Introduced by the United States and co-sponsored by more than 120 Member States; adopted without a vote on 21 March 2024. The second General Assembly resolution on AI that year, 78/311 of 1 July 2024 on capacity-building, is UN-03; both are recalled in resolution 79/325 of 26 August 2025 (UN-07).
