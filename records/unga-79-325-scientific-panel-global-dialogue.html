Summary

Establishes within the UN the Independent International Scientific Panel on Artificial Intelligence, 40 experts serving in their personal capacity who issue an annual evidence-based assessment of the opportunities, risks and impacts of AI and thematic briefs, and the Global Dialogue on AI Governance, a yearly meeting of governments and stakeholders that alternates between Geneva and New York. The Dialogue covers safe, secure and trustworthy AI, capacity gaps, interoperability of governance approaches, human rights, and transparency, accountability and human oversight of AI. Both are limited to the non-military domain.

Status notes

Adopted by consensus at the 89th plenary meeting on 26 August 2025, without reference to a Main Committee (draft A/79/L.118, negotiated in a process co-facilitated by Costa Rica and Spain); the document is dated 27 August 2025. It sets the terms of reference that the Global Digital Compact (UN-05) called for. The Dialogue was launched on 25 September 2025 during the high-level week of the General Assembly's 80th session. The General Assembly appointed the Panel's 40 members in February 2026, and the Panel held its inaugural meeting on 3 March 2026. The first Global Dialogue was held in Geneva on 6 and 7 July 2026 (its Co-Chairs' Summary is UN-13); a further session is planned for New York in 2027. Paragraph 12 lets the General Assembly decide on continuing the terms of reference at the high-level review of the Global Digital Compact at its 82nd session.
