Summary

General Assembly resolution on AI in the military domain that takes up the Secretary-General's report A/80/78. In operative paragraphs 9 and 10 it decides to convene informal exchanges in Geneva, as a three-day meeting in 2026, for States to share perspectives on the report's observations and conclusions, including opportunities and challenges, normative proposals and next steps. The exchanges are open to all Member and observer States, international and regional organizations, the ICRC, academia and civil society, including the scientific and technical community and industry; UNODA supports them and gives a written factual summary to the First Committee at its eighty-first session.
