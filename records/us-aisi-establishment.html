Summary

Announcement of a new U.S. AI Safety Institute, led by the National Institute of Standards and Technology in the Department of Commerce, as part of NIST's response to Executive Order 14110. The institute was to build on the AI Risk Management Framework to improve organizations' ability to evaluate and validate AI systems, partner with other agencies on evaluating AI capabilities, limitations, risks and impacts and on building testbeds, and work with ally and partner countries to share best practices and align capability evaluation and red-team guidance and benchmarks. An AI Safety Institute Consortium supports its work.

Status notes

Announced on 1 November 2023 at the UK's AI Safety Summit 2023, in which Commerce Secretary Gina Raimondo took part, as part of NIST's response to Executive Order 14110 (US-04). On 2 November 2023 NIST called for participants in the AI Safety Institute Consortium, a core element of the institute. The Commerce Secretary's statement of 3 June 2025 transformed the institute into the Center for AI Standards and Innovation (CAISI) within NIST (US-15). By October 2026 NIST presents the center as the Center for Advancing Innovation and Standards for Super Intelligence (CAISSI).
