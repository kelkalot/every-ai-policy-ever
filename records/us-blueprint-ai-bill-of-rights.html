Summary

Non-binding white paper of the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy setting out five principles for automated systems that can meaningfully affect people's rights, opportunities or access to critical resources or services: safe and effective systems; algorithmic discrimination protections; data privacy; notice and explanation; and human alternatives, consideration and fallback. Systems should undergo pre-deployment testing, risk identification and mitigation and ongoing monitoring, with independent evaluation and public reporting whenever possible. A technical companion, From Principles to Practice, sets out how to put the principles into practice.

Status notes

Published by OSTP in October 2022, one year after OSTP announced the process to develop a bill of rights for an AI-powered world. The Blueprint states that it is non-binding, does not constitute U.S. government policy, does not direct the interpretation of any statute or regulation and creates no legal right. It now exists only on the archived website of the Biden White House, marked as historical material that is no longer updated. No instrument formally withdrawing it is known; it is treated as withdrawn in practice.
