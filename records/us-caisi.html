Summary

Reorganises the US AI Safety Institute as CAISI, industry's main government contact for AI testing. Its mandate: develop voluntary guidelines and standards, make voluntary agreements with developers, run unclassified evaluations of cyber, bio and chemical-weapons risks, assess US and adversary systems (including backdoors and foreign influence), coordinate with DoD, DOE, DHS and the Intelligence Community, and oppose burdensome foreign regulation.

Status notes

Announced 3 Jun 2025; the centre is part of NIST. After EO 14434 of 29 Sep 2026 (US-26), NIST presents it as the Center for Advancing Innovation and Standards for Super Intelligence (CAISSI) and its mandate refers to 'SI' systems, while its news items still use the name CAISI. Its published work includes the NIST AI 800-2 draft on automated benchmark evaluations (Jan 2026, US-21); evaluations of DeepSeek V4 Pro (1 May 2026), Z.ai's GLM-5.2 (17 Jul 2026) and GLM-5.3's cyber capabilities (17 Sep 2026); a preliminary assessment of Kimi K3's cyber capabilities with the UK AI Security Institute (23 Jul 2026); and key practices for incident readiness and response in SI training and evaluation (16 Oct 2026). America's AI Action Plan (Jul 2025, US-16) assigns it tasks that include evaluating frontier models. The House 'Great American AI Act' discussion draft (Jun 2026) would codify the centre; it has not been enacted.
