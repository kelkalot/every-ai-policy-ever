Summary

Requires federal agencies to procure only large language models that meet two 'Unbiased AI Principles': truth-seeking and ideological neutrality. OMB must issue implementing guidance that lets vendors show compliance through disclosures (such as system prompts and evaluations) instead of model weights, with exceptions for national security systems.

Status notes

Signed 23 July 2025 and published in the Federal Register on 28 July 2025 (90 FR 35389). It builds on Executive Order 13960 (US-29). OMB had to issue implementing guidance within 120 days and did so in Memorandum M-26-04 of 11 December 2025 (US-19). Agency heads must include in each LLM contract made after that guidance terms requiring compliance with the Unbiased AI Principles, with decommissioning costs charged to the vendor if the agency terminates for non-compliance after a reasonable period to cure; revise existing LLM contracts where practicable; and adopt compliance procedures within 90 days of the guidance. The order creates no right enforceable against the United States.
