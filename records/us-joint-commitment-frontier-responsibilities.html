Summary

Voluntary accord in which six leading AI companies accept that each frontier developer is responsible for its models' safety. They commit to four layers of control: internal monitoring of model capabilities and alignment (focused on cyber, bio and chemical risks and on models hacking or accessing systems), a dedicated internal verification team, an independent external auditor or evaluator, and an independent board committee overseeing remediation. Signatories will also meet regularly to develop safety standards.

Status notes

Signed on 29 Sep 2026 by President Trump and by Sundar Pichai (Google), Dario Amodei (Anthropic), Mark Zuckerberg (Meta), Greg Brockman (OpenAI), Elon Musk (xAI) and Jensen Huang (Nvidia), the day the President signed Executive Order 14434 on 'Super Intelligence' terminology (US-26). The text sets no enforcement or reporting mechanism; each company is to partner with an independent external auditor or evaluator, and the accord says it may make sense over time to codify these steps into laws or regulations. On 1 Oct 2026 Reps. Jay Obernolte and Lori Trahan, authors of the FRONTIER Act (US-25), said the accord embraces principles at the heart of their bill (robust internal risk management, strong corporate governance and independent third-party evaluation) and that the bill would build on it and make it durable.
