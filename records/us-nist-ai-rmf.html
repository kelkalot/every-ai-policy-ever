Summary

Voluntary, rights-preserving, non-sector-specific and use-case agnostic framework, directed by the National Artificial Intelligence Initiative Act of 2020, to help organizations designing, developing, deploying or using AI systems manage AI risks. Part 1 frames AI risks and the characteristics of trustworthy AI: valid and reliable, safe, secure and resilient, accountable and transparent, explainable and interpretable, privacy-enhanced, and fair with harmful biases managed. Part 2, the Core, sets four functions, Govern, Map, Measure and Manage, broken down into categories and subcategories.

Status notes

Released on 26 January 2023 after a request for information (July 2021), a concept paper, two public drafts (17 March and 18 August 2022) and workshops. NIST describes it as a living document, with a review with formal input from the AI community expected no later than 2028. NIST also publishes a companion AI RMF Playbook, a Roadmap and a Crosswalk, and Arabic and Japanese translations. On 26 July 2024 NIST released the Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile (NIST AI 600-1), and on 7 April 2026 a concept note for an AI RMF Profile on Trustworthy AI in Critical Infrastructure. NIST states that the AI RMF 1.0 is being revised as part of the White House AI Action Plan of July 2025, which recommends revising the framework to eliminate references to misinformation, Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion, and climate change.
