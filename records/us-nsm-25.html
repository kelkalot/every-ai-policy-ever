National Security Memorandum 25 (NSM-25)

Summary

Presidential memorandum on AI and national security, with a classified annex. Its three objectives: lead the world's development of safe, secure and trustworthy AI; harness AI with safeguards for national security; and cultivate a stable, responsible framework for international AI governance. It makes the AI Safety Institute the government's primary point of contact with AI developers for voluntary pre- and post-deployment testing of frontier models, including for cyber and biological and chemical weapons risks, and requires a Framework to Advance AI Governance and Risk Management in National Security setting prohibited and high-impact AI uses with minimum risk management practices.

Status notes

Signed by President Biden on 24 Oct 2024. It fulfils the directive in subsection 4.8 of his executive order on AI (EO 14110, US-04). The companion Framework to Advance AI Governance and Risk Management in National Security was published the same day; the memorandum has the NSC Deputies Committee approve the Framework and review it periodically. The White House fact sheet of the same day also says the memorandum makes collection on competitors' operations against the US AI sector a top-tier intelligence priority; the memorandum's public text directs a review of the intelligence priorities to improve the identification of foreign intelligence threats to the US AI ecosystem. NSPM-11 of 5 Jun 2026 (US-28), section 3(f), rescinds and replaces NSM-25 and associated guidance.
