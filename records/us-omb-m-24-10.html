Summary

OMB memorandum directing federal agencies to strengthen AI governance, advance responsible AI innovation and manage risks from their use of AI. Each agency had to designate a Chief AI Officer within 60 days, and CFO Act agencies had to develop AI strategies. By 1 December 2024 agencies had to apply minimum risk-management practices to safety-impacting and rights-impacting AI, including an AI impact assessment, real-world testing, independent evaluation, ongoing monitoring, operator training and human oversight, and public notice, plus notice to negatively affected individuals and opt-out options for rights-impacting AI, or else stop using the AI.

Status notes

Issued on 28 March 2024 consistent with the AI in Government Act of 2020, the Advancing American AI Act and Executive Order 14110, after a draft published for public comment on 1 November 2023. It governs only agencies' own use of AI and does not create rights or obligations for the public; Sections 5(a) to 5(c) do not apply to elements of the Intelligence Community. Rescinded and replaced on 3 April 2025 by OMB Memorandum M-25-21 (US-12), issued after Executive Order 14179 of 23 January 2025. Its companion acquisition memorandum M-24-18 was likewise rescinded and replaced by M-25-22 of the same day.
