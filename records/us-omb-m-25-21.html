Summary

Replaces M-24-10 as the government-wide policy on agency AI use. It defines 'high-impact AI', meaning AI whose output is a principal basis for decisions with legal, material or significant effects on rights or safety. For such AI it requires minimum practices: pre-deployment testing, AI impact assessments with independent review, ongoing monitoring, operator training, human oversight and fail-safes, and human review and appeal. Non-compliant uses must be discontinued, waivers require CAIO certification, and Chief AI Officers and governance boards remain.

Status notes

Issued 3 Apr 2025 under EO 14179. Deadlines from issuance: CAIO within 60 days; CFO Act agency AI strategies and agency compliance plans within 180 days (repeating every two years until 2036); high-impact minimum practices documented within 365 days (about 3 Apr 2026). In force as of Oct 2026. Binding on federal agencies only.
