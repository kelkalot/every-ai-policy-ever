Summary

OMB memorandum implementing EO 14319. Every agency solicitation or order for a large language model issued after it must include contract requirements on compliance with the Unbiased AI Principles of truth-seeking and ideological neutrality, and agencies must request at least the vendor's acceptable use policy, model, system or data cards, end-user resources and a feedback mechanism. Agencies may ask for more, such as bias evaluation results and pre- and post-training details, and their procurement policies must let users report outputs that violate the principles. National security systems are excluded.

Status notes

Issued 11 December 2025 under section 4 of EO 14319 (US-17); it complements OMB M-25-22 (US-13). Agencies had to update their procurement policies by 11 March 2026 and should modify existing LLM contracts, where practicable, before exercising any option that extends them. Agencies need not apply it to LLMs acquired under a free, open-source licence but should check such models against the principles and against M-25-21 (US-12) before use. Agencies should make the transparency requirements material to eligibility and payment, to support termination for default. The memorandum ceases to have effect two years after its issuance (11 December 2027) unless the OMB Director provides otherwise.
