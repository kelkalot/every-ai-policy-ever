Summary

Voluntary commitments by Amazon, Anthropic, Google, Inflection, Meta, Microsoft and OpenAI, secured by the White House, to internal and external red-teaming of models before release (including biological, chemical, radiological and cyber risks), information sharing on safety risks with companies and governments, protecting unreleased model weights, third-party discovery and reporting of vulnerabilities, provenance or watermarking for AI-generated audio and visual content, public reports on capabilities and limitations, and research on societal risks. They cover generative models more powerful than the industry frontier at the time.

Status notes

Announced on 21 July 2023 at the White House. The commitments rest on three principles, safety, security and trust, and the companies intended them to remain in effect until regulations covering substantially the same issues come into force. The White House said it had consulted on them with 20 countries and linked them to the G7 Hiroshima Process and the UK AI Safety Summit. The fact sheet now exists only on the archived Biden White House website. The Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities of 29 September 2026 (US-27) replaced them as the federal government's voluntary framework with frontier AI companies.
