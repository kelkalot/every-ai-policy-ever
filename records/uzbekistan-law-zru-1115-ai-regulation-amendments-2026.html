Summary

Amending law that writes AI into Uzbekistan's Law on Informatization and Code of Administrative Responsibility. It defines artificial intelligence as technological solutions that imitate human knowledge and skills, including independent learning and searching for solutions, and adds Article 7-1: AI-made information resources and AI-based systems must not harm a person's life, health, freedom, honour, dignity or other inalienable rights, and legally significant decisions affecting human rights and freedoms may not rest solely on their conclusions. Unlawful processing of personal data using AI, and its dissemination in the media or online, is fined 50 to 100 base calculation units.

Status notes

Adopted by the Legislative Chamber on 12 August 2025, approved by the Senate on 1 November 2025, signed on 21 January 2026 and in force from its official publication (Article 4), which lex.uz dates 21 January 2026. The amendments also give the competent state body AI tasks: attracting investment in AI, building technical infrastructure to process government data with AI, and training staff. Article 3 instructs the Cabinet of Ministers to bring government decisions into line with the law. lex.uz publishes a Russian text marked as an unofficial translation.
