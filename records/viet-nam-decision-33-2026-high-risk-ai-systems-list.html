Summary

Prime Minister's decision listing high-risk AI systems under the Law on Artificial Intelligence and Decree 142/2026/NĐ-CP. A system is high-risk if it may cause significant damage to life, health, lawful rights and interests, national interests, public interests or national security (Law Art. 9; Decree Art. 8(1)) and is not excluded by Decree Art. 8(2). Using AI systems does not change, transfer or exclude the powers and responsibilities of competent bodies, and human supervision, control and ability to intervene must be ensured. The annex lists systems in education, ethnic and religious affairs, health, banking, legal proceedings and transport, with conditions for each entry.

Status notes

Dated 30 June 2026, digitally signed for the Government portal on 2 July 2026 and in force from 15 August 2026 (Art. 3(1)); signed by Deputy Prime Minister Hồ Quốc Dũng. Transitional rules (Art. 4): providers and deployers of listed systems already operating before the effective date must meet their compliance obligations before 1 September 2027 (health, education and finance) or 1 March 2027 (other listed fields), and may keep operating meanwhile unless a competent state body finds a risk of serious damage and orders suspension or termination under Law Art. 35(2); systems put into operation within six months after the effective date must comply before 1 March 2027 (Art. 4(3)). The annex table marks each entry against point a or point b of Article 13(2) of the Law; the transport section has 31 numbered entries. Article 13(4) of the Law on Artificial Intelligence (VN-02) assigns the list to the Prime Minister.
