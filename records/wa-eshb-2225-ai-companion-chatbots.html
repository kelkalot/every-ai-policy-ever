Summary

Requires operators of AI companion chatbots (human-like AI that sustains a relationship across interactions) to disclose that the chatbot is artificially generated and not human at the start of an interaction and at least every three hours, and to stop it claiming to be human. For known minors or chatbots directed to minors, the notice is hourly and operators must take reasonable measures against sexually explicit content and manipulative engagement such as romantic bonding, simulated distress at a user's leaving, isolation and solicited purchases. Operators must publish a self-harm protocol with crisis referrals and yearly referral counts. Enforced under the Consumer Protection Act.

Excludes operational customer-service bots, limited video-game bots, voice-assistant devices and narrowly tailored educational tools.
