Summary

Cabinet-approved draft policy for public comment, proposing an EU-inspired risk-based regime phased in from unacceptable-risk to high-risk requirements, a National AI Safety Institute, an AI Regulatory Authority, an AI Ethics Board, an AI Ombudsperson, an AI Insurance Superfund, human-rights impact assessments, bias testing, explainability duties and remedies against deepfakes. It was withdrawn in full two months after publication.

Status notes

Dated March 2026; approved by Cabinet on 25 March 2026 (combined with a special sitting of Cabinet on 1 April 2026), signed by the Minister on 9 April 2026 and gazetted on 10 April 2026 for 60 days of public comment, to 10 June 2026. It was based on the South Africa National Artificial Intelligence Policy Framework of August 2024 (ZA-02), the 32 submissions received on it and consultations through the Cabinet cluster process, and set out staged implementation: draft requirements for unacceptable risks in year 1 (2025/26), requirements for high-risk use cases in year 2 (2026/27) and full implementation in year 3 (2027/28). It was withdrawn in its entirety by Notice 3978 of 2026 (Government Gazette No. 54840), signed on 4 June 2026 and gazetted on 12 June 2026, with no reason given.
